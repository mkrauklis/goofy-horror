Halloween 1 — The Candy Maze

Collect all 3 buckets of candy and the exit opens — but the thing wearing the pumpkin can see you from across the maze.

Candy: 0 / 3 Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

It's Halloween in the facility. The floors have gone orange, there are jack-o-lanterns tucked into every corner, and the windows are lit up like candles — which is the only light you'll get besides your flashlight. The whole place has been turned into a maze: long, narrow corridors with walls between them, and the three buckets of candy are sitting at the far ends of it. Grab all three and the exit door opens.

The Hollow Jack lives in here: a mass of tentacles wearing a pumpkin with eyeholes carved out of it. It wanders the maze at 0.8x your speed, but its eyesight is excellent — if you're in a straight line of sight anywhere in a corridor it sees you, no matter where your flashlight is pointing, and then it hunts you down at 1.6x your speed. Break line of sight, hide in a ventilation duct (it can't follow you in), or run for your safe room. Item crates are scattered around the maze with the usual radar, scanner, meat, CO2 and the rest.

Each of you is carrying a tranquilizer gun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can carry up to 3 at a time) and each one loads automatically — let something get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 5 seconds.
