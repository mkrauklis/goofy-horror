Level 25 — The Spore Engine

A grassy, window-lined boss arena, the whole thing visible on screen at once. The Cultivator — a 3x3 robot grown over with grass and crowned with one giant mushroom — has 25 HP. Find coal scattered around the room and feed the furnace on the south wall, one damage per coal, to bring it down.

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

A wide, grassy arena with windows on all four walls, the whole room visible on screen at once — nothing to scroll past, every attack readable the moment it starts. Coal piles respawn around the room's edges; carry one to the furnace built into the south wall and it costs the boss 1 of its 25 HP. There's no other way to hurt it.

Four attacks while it's healthy. Gear throw: a gear launches at whoever's closest every 2 seconds for 10 seconds straight, flying at 2x your speed and ricocheting off the walls instead of stopping. Spore bloom: it shakes its head and roughly 20 spores drift out from the mushroom, floating for 3 seconds before landing, swelling into mushrooms of their own, and bursting in a blast about 2 tiles across. Mycelium chase: both extendo-arms plant into the ground and tunnel after whoever's nearest at 1.3x your speed, leaving a trail of mounds behind each hand — the mounds themselves are harmless, only the hand is lethal. Rocket cannon: a cannon rises from its back and fires 8 rockets in a spread, each one exploding the instant it hits you, a wall, or a crate.

At 15 HP its grass turns yellow, its plating starts rusting, and it gains two more attacks on top of the first four. Fire breath: its mouth opens and a jet of fire reaches 5 tiles out in whatever direction it's facing. Stalk walk: every window in the room seals shut over 1 second, the mushroom starts flashing neon green every 1.5 seconds, and it bears straight down on whoever's nearest at 1.4x your speed — while it does, your own flashlight shrinks to barely a tile around you and the boss drops off your minimap entirely.

Each of you is carrying a tranquilizer gun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (up to 3 at a time) and it loads automatically — get close enough while it's bearing down on you during stalk walk and it goes off on its own, stunning it for a few seconds.
