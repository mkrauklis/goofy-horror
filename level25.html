Level 25 — The Spore Engine

A grassy, window-lined boss arena, the whole thing visible on screen at once. The Cultivator — a round 3x3 robot, real grass growing out of its hull, crowned with one giant mushroom — has 25 HP. Carry one coal at a time from around the room to the furnace on the south wall, one damage per coal, to bring it down. If one of you falls, you're down for the rest of the run — the fight's only over once everyone has.

A wide, grassy arena with windows on all four walls, the whole room visible on screen at once — nothing to scroll past, every attack readable the moment it starts. Coal piles respawn around the room's edges, but you can only ever carry one at a time; walk it to the furnace built into the south wall and it costs the boss 1 of its 25 HP. There's no other way to hurt it — the freeze item does nothing against this one.

Five attacks while it's healthy. Gear throw: a gear launches at whoever's closest every 2 seconds for 10 seconds straight, flying at 2x your speed and ricocheting off the walls instead of stopping. Spore bloom: it shakes its head and roughly 20 spores scatter out across the whole arena, floating for 3 seconds before landing, swelling into mushrooms of their own, and bursting in a blast about 2 tiles across. Mycelium chase: both extendo-arms plant into the ground and tunnel after whoever's nearest at 1.3x your speed, leaving a trail of mounds behind each hand — the mounds themselves are harmless, only the hand is lethal. Rocket cannon: a cannon rises from its back and fires 8 rockets in a spread, each one exploding the instant it hits you, a wall, or a crate. Magnet pull: its right arm ends in a magnet instead of a claw, and it locks onto whoever's nearest, hauling them in at 0.8x your speed for 7 seconds straight — get dragged into actual contact with it and that's lethal too.

At 15 HP its grass turns yellow, its plating starts rusting, the spore bloom now throws 10 extra spores on top of the usual 20, and it gains two more attacks. Fire breath: its mouth opens and a jet of fire reaches 5 tiles out in whatever direction it's facing. Stalk walk: every window in the room seals shut over 1 second, the mushroom starts flashing pitch black to neon green every 2 seconds, and it bears straight down on whoever's nearest at a steady 1x your speed — while it does, your own flashlight shrinks to barely a tile around you and the boss drops off your minimap entirely.

Each of you is carrying a tranquilizer gun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (up to 3 at a time) and it loads automatically — get close enough while it's bearing down on you during stalk walk and it goes off on its own, stunning it for a few seconds.
