Level 8 — The Deep Vault

Put out all 5 blue torches to unlock the vault door — a huge, armored, pale green thing patrols these oversized halls, faster than you and watching from twice the usual distance, plus three smaller chain-wielding things your own size.

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

Everything down here keeps growing — the halls, the rooms, all of it, doubled in each dimension yet again on top of the last resize — and the thing that lives in them hasn't shrunk to match: a pale green cousin of the very first Crawler you ever ran from, except this one is four times its size, bolted into armor plate, and faster than either of you (2.5x your speed). It notices you from twice the distance a normal creature would. There's no lever, no fuse box this time — just five blue torches burning somewhere in these cavernous rooms. Put out all five and the vault door unlocks — but so does the last of the torchlight: your own flashlight only reaches a quarter as far once the vault goes properly dark. The rooms are stacked with loot crates, and a few solid 2x2 crates besides, for cover if nothing else.

Three smaller things share these halls with it — solid black or stark white, your own size, each swinging a chained ball around itself as it moves. Only 1.1x your speed, so outrunning one isn't the problem; there just isn't much room to put between you and something that size in a hall built for the Guardian.
