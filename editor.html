Designer

Build the new levels and mini bosses: paint the map, shape the monster, set how it behaves. Saved in this browser, playable straight away.

Mode

    Left-drag paints the selected tile, right-drag erases to floor. Player spawns (1, 2) and the exit (X) can only exist once — placing another moves it. Monster spawns (M) may be several. Patrol points (P) are walked in the order they appear, reading the map left to right, top to bottom. Doors (D) stay shut until every key is taken (levels) or the boss falls (mini bosses). Lava (L) burns anything that walks on it. Bombs (B) are where a mini boss’s bombs can appear.

    Drag a shape in the preview to move it. The eyes follow your pointer.
