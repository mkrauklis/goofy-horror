Halloween 5 — The Pumpkin King

A round arena floored with the ward's tiles, vines creeping across it and jack-o-lanterns glowing on the ground, the whole thing visible at once. The Pumpkin King — a huge 4.5x4.5 orange pumpkin with a fire burning in its mouth — has 50 HP. Every 5 attacks it's stunned for 7.5 seconds: walk up and hit it for 2.5 damage. At 30 HP it flourishes: it turns green, grows more vines and its fire burns blue. If one of you falls you stay down until everybody does.

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

The final Halloween room: a huge, perfectly round arena, floored with the same mottled tiles as the ward and ringed by a wall of red brick. Vines have crept across the whole floor, and dozens of jack-o-lanterns sit glowing on the ground — they're the only light in here. Nothing hides in the dark, though: the whole arena is on screen at every moment, so every attack is readable the moment it starts. The Pumpkin King leaves a trail of glowing embers wherever it goes, and the trail slowly fades away.

You can only hurt it one way. Every 5th attack it ends up stunned for 7.5 seconds — its mouth-fire gutters down to embers — and while it's down you walk up to it and hit it, which does 2.5 damage (each of you can hit again about a second later). Touch it any other time and you're done.

Its attacks: 1. the fire spiral — it spins and spits fireballs out of its mouth at random at 5x your speed for 10 seconds, every one twice the normal size. 2. the roar — its mouth gapes and blazes, then it runs you down at 1.2x your speed for 10 seconds. 3. eruption — ten round 2x2 patches flash red for a second, then a pillar of fire shoots up out of the floor. 4. the vine whirl — it ricochets off the walls at 2x your speed for 10 seconds, swinging 5-tile-long vines round itself that kill on touch.

At 30 HP it flourishes: it turns green, sprouts a much bigger nest of leafy vines (the vine whirl swings seven instead of four), and the fire in it turns blue — and it learns two new attacks. 5. the thorn burst: it curls up all its vines (a red circle on the floor shows how far it'll reach), then a shockwave of thorn vines bursts out 6 tiles in every direction around it — get outside the circle before it goes off. 6. spores: three 5x5 clouds of poisonous spores appear (you get a 1-second warning of where). Stay in one for more than 2.5 seconds and you die; get out sooner and you're only slowed by 30% for 5 seconds. If one of you falls, you stay down for the rest of the fight — it's only over once everyone has. Crates are still scattered around, with the radar, CO2 and the rest.
