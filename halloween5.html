Halloween 5 — The Candy Kraken

A round arena with a floor made entirely of candy, walled in with brick, the whole thing visible at once. The Candy Kraken — a 2x2 tentacled monster made of candy — has 50 HP. Every 5 attacks it's stunned for 7.5 seconds: run up and bite it for 10 damage. At 30 HP it withers and gains a new attack. If one of you falls you stay down until everybody does.

The final Halloween room: a perfectly round arena, candy underfoot wherever you look — glossy tiles, sprinkles, peppermint disks — ringed by a wall of red brick, jack-o-lanterns glowing around the edge. There's no darkness here: the whole arena is on screen at every moment, so every attack is readable the moment it starts. The Candy Kraken leaves a trail of candy wherever it goes, and the trail slowly fades away.

You can only hurt it one way. Every 5th attack it ends up stunned for 7.5 seconds — and while it's down you run up and take a bite out of it, which does 10 damage (each of you can bite again about a second later). Touch it any other time and you're done.

Its attacks: 1. the candy spiral — it spins and fires candy out at random at 5x your speed for 10 seconds, every piece twice the normal size. 2. the scream — it screams, then runs you down at 1.3x your speed for 10 seconds. 3. candy corn — ten round 2x2 patches flash red for a second, then candy corn shoots up out of the floor.

At 30 HP it enters its withered phase: chunks missing, ribcage showing — and it learns a fourth attack, cotton candy. Three 5x5 clouds appear (you get a 1-second warning of where). Stay in one for more than 2.5 seconds and you die; get out sooner and you're only slowed by 30% for 5 seconds. If one of you falls, you stay down for the rest of the fight — it's only over once everyone has. Crates are still scattered around, with the radar, CO2 and the rest.
