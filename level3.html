Level 3 — Sinkhole

Hold both players on the generator's pressure plates for 5 seconds — and stay off the meat vines, or the caterpillar comes running.

Generator: charging 0% Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

Old floor tiles have crumbled away to bare dirt all through this facility. Get both players onto the two pressure plates in the generator room at the same time and hold for 5 seconds to power up the door. A slow caterpillar-like creature barely sees past its own body — but meat vines are strung thick across the ground (there's almost always one somewhere on screen), and stepping on one sends it rocketing at 20 times its normal speed straight to that spot. Item crates can turn up a radar, a scanner pointing at the generator, a distracting slab of meat, or a CO2 blast that freezes it solid.

Each of you is carrying a shotgun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can only hold one at a time) and it loads automatically — let something get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 3 seconds.
