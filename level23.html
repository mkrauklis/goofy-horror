Same decor as the wing next door — moss and grass over 90% of the floor, wooden roots threading the gaps, windows the only fixed light besides your own. Laid out completely differently though: two big sections, one for spawn and one for the exit, joined by a single long hallway with windows down both walls and shelving lining it on either side.

No lever, no item, no door to unlock this time — the exit is already open. Just get there.

Three Stalkers patrol the dark at 1.8x your speed. Stay out of their line of sight and they'll wander on by — but once one spots you, it gives chase at 2.5x your speed until it loses you again. Your safe zone is still safe: duck into it and no Stalker can see you there, no matter how close it already was.
