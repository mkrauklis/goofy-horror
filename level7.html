Level 7 — The Mine

Find the lever to launch the minecart down its rail — it'll break the wooden door open on its own. A fifth of these rooms have lost their floor; stick to the boardwalk, because the thing patrolling down here doesn't need one.

Lever: not pulled Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

Same cobblestone, same wall torches, same stone statues as the Undercroft above — except a fifth of these rooms have lost their floor to an old chasm, with nothing but a wooden boardwalk laid straight across. A grey, wet-winged thing lives down here, stone wings folded against its back and a mouth where its eye should be: it has no trouble crossing the chasm itself, so the boardwalk is yours alone, and it's fast enough (2x your speed) to make that matter. There's no bank of switches this time — just one lever, rigged to a mine cart sitting idle on its rail. Pull it and the cart runs the line on its own, straight into the wooden door sealing the way out, and breaks it open.

Each of you is carrying a shotgun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can only hold one at a time) and it's loaded automatically — let the thing get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 3 seconds. No button to press; just don't walk in without one.
