Level 7 — The Mine

Find the lever to launch the minecart down its rail — it'll break the wooden door open on its own. A fifth of these rooms have lost their floor; stick to the boardwalk, because the four things patrolling down here don't need one. They don't move while the torches are lit, either — just watch for when they go dark.

Twenty times the floor space of the original dig, and it shows — a long run of cobblestone halls, one of which has lost its floor to an old chasm entirely, with nothing but a wooden boardwalk laid straight across. Four grey, wet-winged things live down here, stone wings folded against their backs and a mouth where an eye should be, and they have no trouble crossing the chasm themselves, so the boardwalk is yours alone. There's no bank of switches this time — just one lever, rigged to a mine cart sitting idle on its rail. Pull it and the cart runs the line on its own, straight into the wooden door sealing the way out, and breaks it open.

The torch sconces lining these halls run on their own clock, nothing to do with you: lit for 4 seconds, dark for 2, forever. While they're lit, the things down here don't move at all — frozen wherever they happen to be standing. The instant the lights cut, they close any distance at 10x your speed. Four seconds to watch and plan, two seconds where standing still is the worst thing you can do.

A second cart rides a closed loop in its own room, on its own track — silver instead of the lever cart's wood and iron, circling endlessly at a lazy 1.2x your speed. It isn't wired to anything and it isn't going anywhere; it was just already running when the dig was abandoned, and nobody's stopped it since.

Each of you is carrying a shotgun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can only hold one at a time) and it's loaded automatically — let the thing get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 3 seconds. No button to press; just don't walk in without one.
