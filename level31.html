Level 31 — The Way Out

Broad daylight, out front of the facility, a car parked in the lot. Everyone gets in — and the moment you do, the doors burst open and the Multipod comes out after you: every monster you've met, fused into one screaming swarm, running you down at 3x your walking speed. The car matches it. Drive the ring road around the grounds and keep firing missiles over your shoulder — it has 500 HP and every missile takes off 25. Let it catch you and that's the end of the road.

On foot — Player 1 (orange): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green): Arrow keys. In the car — drive with W A S D (W gas, S brake/reverse), fire a missile with Space (Player 2: Up Arrow or Space). Solo, the arrow keys drive too. · sound plays after your first key press

No darkness this time. The sun's out, the lawn's mowed, and the facility you spent thirty levels getting through is just a big grey building behind a parking lot. A red car sits in the lot with the keys in it. Walk everyone up to it — it won't go until all of you are standing there.

The car does 3x your walking speed. Stay on the asphalt: the grass drags you down to half speed, trees and the building stop you dead, and traffic cones along the ring road just get knocked flat (at a cost to your speed). One of you drives; the other fires. Playing alone, you do both.

The Multipod is every creature in this game at once — drifters, gargoyles, wardens, grotesques, reachers, caterpillars, robots, mushrooms and the rest — one swarm, 500 HP, chasing at 3x. It follows your exact tire tracks, so it never cuts a corner, but any time you slow down it closes the gap for good. Missiles home in on it at 5x walking speed for 25 damage each — twenty hits ends it, and every hit knocks it back down your trail.
