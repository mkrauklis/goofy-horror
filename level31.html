Level 31 — The Way Out

Broad daylight, out front of the facility, a car parked in the lot for each of you. Everyone gets in — and the moment you do, the doors burst open and the Multipod comes out after you: every monster you've met, bosses included, fused into one swarm, running you down at 3x your walking speed. One straight road, a forest that swallows the light, and the only way out is to bring the Multipod down. It has 500 HP, shown on a big bar at the top of the screen and on a smaller one riding on the swarm itself; every missile takes off 25 (12.5 each when there are two of you).

No darkness at first. The sun's out, the lawn's mowed, and the facility you spent thirty levels getting through is just a big grey building behind a parking lot. A car sits waiting for each of you, already pointed down the one road out. Walk up to yours — it won't go until everyone's in.

The cars do 3x your walking speed. Stay on the asphalt: the grass drags you down to half speed, and the trees and the building stop you dead. Past the treeline the forest is dense enough to be a wall on both sides — and it gets darker the deeper you go, until the only light is your headlights. Traffic cones get knocked flat, but clip one and you're capped at half speed for 3 seconds. Playing alone, you drive and fire both; with two, one of you drives each car and each fires their own missiles.

The Multipod is every creature in this game at once, drawn with their real models — drifters, wardens, grotesques, the Cistern, the Last Knight, the Garguantuagoyle and the rest. It stays pinned to the left edge of the screen — the view pulls back to keep your lead car in frame when you pull ahead — and it runs at your rear car at 3x, and if it falls behind it sprints up to 5x to close the gap. Every 2.5 seconds it also hurls one of the projectiles from this book at you — a gear, a rocket, an icicle, a feather, antimatter, a coral ball, a spore, a chain ball, a slug, a chunk of floor — at 6x your walking speed, aimed where you're about to be. One hit and that car is destroyed. Missiles home in on it at 5x walking speed and knock it back; if the last car goes, so does the run.

But killing it isn't the end of it. The blast wrecks your car, and the Multipod comes apart into the individual monsters — every one of them chasing you on foot as fast as you can run. You run at 2x with no stamina limit, but the road now winds through the forest and is dotted with 2x1-tile concrete roadblocks you have to weave around — every bend and every dodge lets them gain. Get to the military roadblock and through the gap in the sandbags. Get caught and you retry the run from the crash. Pass the soldiers and they open fire.
