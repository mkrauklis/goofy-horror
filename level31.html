Level 31 — The Way Out

Broad daylight, out front of the facility, a car parked in the lot for each of you. Everyone gets in — and the moment you do, the doors burst open and the Multipod comes out after you: every monster you've met, bosses included, fused into one swarm, running you down at 3x your walking speed. One straight road, a forest that swallows the light, and no finish line — it doesn't end until the Multipod does. It has 500 HP; every missile takes off 25 (12.5 each when there are two of you).

Two players: Player 1 drives with W A S D and fires with Space; Player 2 drives with the arrows and fires with Enter. · sound plays after your first key press

No darkness at first. The sun's out, the lawn's mowed, and the facility you spent thirty levels getting through is just a big grey building behind a parking lot. A car sits waiting for each of you, already pointed down the one road out. Walk up to yours — it won't go until everyone's in.

The cars do 3x your walking speed. Stay on the asphalt: the grass drags you down to half speed, and the trees and the building stop you dead. Past the treeline the forest is dense enough to be a wall on both sides — and it gets darker the deeper you go, until the only light is your headlights. Traffic cones just get knocked flat, at a cost to your speed. Playing alone, you drive and fire both; with two, one of you drives each car and each fires their own missiles.

The Multipod is every creature in this game at once, drawn with their real models — drifters, wardens, grotesques, the Cistern, the Last Knight, the Garguantuagoyle and the rest. It stays pinned to the left edge of the screen — the view pulls back to keep your lead car in frame when you pull ahead — and it runs at your rear car at 3x, and if it falls behind it sprints up to 5x to close the gap. Every 2.5 seconds it also hurls one of the projectiles from this book at you — a gear, a rocket, an icicle, a feather, antimatter, a coral ball, a spore, a chain ball, a slug, a chunk of floor — at 6x your walking speed, aimed where you're about to be. One hit and that car is destroyed. Missiles home in on it at 5x walking speed and knock it back; if the last car goes, so does the run.
