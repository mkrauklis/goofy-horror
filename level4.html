Level 4 — Overgrown

Pull the electrical room's lever and get both players aboard the train — watch for the thing wandering the halls that looks just like a person.

Power: off Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

The same facility as Level 3, years later and noticeably bigger, with more dead-end side corridors to get turned around in — vegetation has swallowed the corridors (on top of the same patches of missing floor), and a good number of fires burn permanently once you find them. Find the electrical room (wrapped in exposed wiring so it's unmistakable) and pull its lever — either player can do it alone — to power up the train, then get both players aboard. Three mimics disguise themselves as people and wander the halls at close to your own pace — get too close and the act drops instantly, shrinking into a small toothy tentacle thing that charges at 2x your speed. Item crates are everywhere here, restocking twice as fast as other levels, and a few are secretly mimic crates in disguise.

Each of you is carrying a shotgun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can carry up to 3 at a time) and each one loads automatically — let something get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 5 seconds.
