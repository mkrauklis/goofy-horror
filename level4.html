GOOFY HORROR

Level 4 — Overgrown. The same facility as Level 3, gone to seed: vegetation has swallowed the corridors (on top of the same patches of missing floor), and every now and then you'll catch a fire burning somewhere in the dark before it dies back down. Find the electrical room and pull the lever to get the train running, then get both players aboard. Something wanders the halls that looks exactly like a person — get too close and it drops the act, shrinking into a small toothy tentacle thing that charges at twice your speed.
