Level 24 — The Gearworks

Same moss, grass and wooden roots as the wing next door, now with wildflowers growing through it and slow conveyor belts cutting across both sections. Spin all 3 gears (5 seconds held at each) to open the exit. Two Sentries hunt by sight, same traits as Level 22's Kindling but 15% slower — each one firing its own shotgun at the closest player every 5 seconds.

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

Same decor as the wing next door — moss and grass over 90% of the floor, wooden roots threading the gaps, windows the only fixed light besides your own — except wildflowers now grow up through the grass too, and a few conveyor belts run across each section, carrying anyone standing on them along at half your walking speed.

No lever, no item this time — 3 gears, one in each section and one in the hallway between them. Stand at one for a full 5 seconds to spin it; step away and it slowly winds back down. Spin all 3 and the exit opens.

Two Sentries — tentacled, gear-studded machines with one red eye — hunt the same way Level 22's Kindling does, just 15% slower: always closing the instant they can see you, no slow patrol and fast charge to it. Each one also carries a shotgun of its own, firing at whichever of you it can currently see that's closest every 5 seconds, the blast covering ground at 3x your speed.
