Level 24 — The Gearworks

Same moss, grass and wooden roots as the wing next door, now with wildflowers growing through it and slow conveyor belts cutting across both sections. Spin all 3 gears (5 seconds held at each) to open the exit. Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

Same decor as the wing next door — moss and grass over 90% of the floor, wooden roots threading the gaps, windows the only fixed light besides your own — except wildflowers now grow up through the grass too, and a few conveyor belts run across each section, carrying anyone standing on them along at half your walking speed.

No lever, no item this time — 3 gears, one in each section and one in the hallway between them. Stand at one for a full 5 seconds to spin it; step away and it slowly winds back down. Spin all 3 and the exit opens.

Two Sentries — tentacled, gear-studded machines with one red eye — hold their ground and sweep a laser around themselves in a slow circle. Walk through it once and it fires a missile straight at you at 3x your speed. Walk through it again and the Sentry itself tears free and charges at 1.5x your speed until it catches you or loses the trail.
