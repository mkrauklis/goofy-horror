GOOFY HORROR

Level 2 — Blackout. The power's still out, but this time there's no button panel — find the fuse and seat it in the one fuse box in the facility that's actually wired to the door (follow the wire once you spot it). Two creatures patrol the dark slowly and can't find you at all if you hold still — but move while one is close and it charges at eight times your speed. Item crates can turn up a radar, a distracting slab of meat, or a CO2 blast that freezes a creature solid.

Fuse: on the ground Door: locked
