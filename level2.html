Level 2 — Blackout

Find the fuse and seat it in the one real fuse box — hold still whenever a creature's close, since they hunt by sound alone.

Fuse: on the ground Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

The power's still out, but this time there's no button panel — find the fuse and seat it in the one fuse box in the facility that's actually wired to the door (follow the wire once you spot it). Two creatures patrol the dark slowly and can't find you at all if you hold still — but move while one is close and it charges at eight times your speed. Item crates can turn up a radar, a scanner pointing at the fuse (or the real box once it's picked up), a distracting slab of meat, or a CO2 blast that freezes a creature solid.

Each of you is carrying a shotgun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can only hold one at a time) and it loads automatically — let something get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 3 seconds.
