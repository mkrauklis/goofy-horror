Level 10 — The Last Knight

Survive the knight's four attacks, wait for it to stun itself every 5th attack, then land a dynamite hit — 50 HP, 3 damage per stunned hit. Halfway down, it turns on you harder.

Boss: 50/50 HP Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

A wide stone arena, a little bigger than the Cinder Pit's own, with the whole thing visible on screen at once — no fog of war, no hunting for a path, just you, your partner, and a knight in dark ruby and iron, giant sword in hand, filling most of it.

Four attacks, cycling forever: a straight-line charge at 5x your speed, sword pointed dead ahead; the blade turning red hot while the knight slowly circles, swinging it in a fast continuous arc; a burst of broken floor tiles shot outward in every direction at once at 3x speed; and both tentacles extended all the way out, slowly sweeping around at 1x speed — ducking behind a wall still stops one cold. Every 5th attack, it's stunned for 10 seconds — find a dynamite stick (they spawn randomly around the arena) and land it then for 3 damage, or 1 any other time.

Drop it to 25 HP and the rubies in its armor turn blue, start throwing off fire of their own, and it moves 1.5x faster across every attack above. Finish it off and it freezes solid where it falls, ice forming over the armor, until a blast of snow carries both of you away.
