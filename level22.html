Level 22 — The Greenhouse

A different wing of the same overgrown facility, moss and grass and wooden roots everywhere, windows along the outer walls finally letting real light in. Find the lighter in the storage room and burn through the vines choking the one way out — something brown and twig-covered is hunting with a shotgun of its own.

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

Same moss, grass and wooden roots as the wing next door, but laid out completely differently — a sprawling, irregular building of randomly-sized rooms linked by winding corridors instead of a clean grid, with exactly one path to any given room. Windows punched into the outer walls are a fixed light source of their own, a real beam spilling a few tiles into whichever room they sit in, on top of whatever your own flashlight reaches. A handful of rooms also have solid 2x2 crates blocking line of sight — real cover, not just set dressing.

A lighter is sitting in the facility's storage room. Find it and carry it to the one way out, choked floor to ceiling in vines — get close while still carrying it and the vines go up in flame.

Five creatures patrol the dark — the same sight-hunting cousin of the Crawler from Level 1, sticks jutting out of its hide and its whole body gone brown. It doesn't bother with a slow patrol and a fast charge: it's always closing at a steady 1.1x your speed the instant it can see you. Worse, it's carrying its own shotgun — every 5 seconds it fires at whichever of you it can currently see that's closest, the bullet itself covering ground at 3x your speed.

Item crates can still turn up a radar, a scanner pointing at the lighter (or the vine gate once it's picked up), a distracting slab of meat, or a CO2 blast that freezes a creature solid. Each of you is also carrying your own tranquilizer gun, empty — find a shell in a crate (up to 3 at a time) and it loads automatically, stunning anything that gets close while hunting you for 5 seconds.
