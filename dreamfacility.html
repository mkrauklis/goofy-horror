The Facility

It looks like any other facility. It is not. The floor and the walls will not stay what they are — and this time you are the monster. Everyone in the building has to die.

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

Tile, then dirt, then grass, then cloud, then water, snow, obsidian, and round again — the floor and the walls out of step with one another. There are people here. They can feel you coming, and they run. A red mark at the edge of your light points toward the nearest of them.

Every so often you will flicker, for a moment, into something you would rather not be.
