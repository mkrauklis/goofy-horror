The Green Field

A field with nothing in it but grass, flowers and 25 little green men. They won't hurt you. Go and say hello.

Leprechauns: 0 / 25

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

Nobody is hunting you here. There is no door, no button and no timer: just a very large, very green field, and twenty-five leprechauns standing about in it the way they have always stood, wandering about and hopping a little. They don't chase.

They do seem to notice you, though.
