Level 11 — The Frost

Get all 3 generators online — both players holding down a generator's pair of pressure plates at once charges it — to open the door. Icicles litter the floor and snow drifts past in the dark.

Generators: 0 / 3 online Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

A frozen stretch of the facility, icicles hanging off the ceiling into frame throughout and a steady drift of snow past the flashlight beam. Three separate generator rooms are scattered through it, each with its own pair of pressure plates — get both of you onto a room's plates at once and hold for 5 seconds to bring that generator online. All three have to be lit before the door out opens.

A pale blue tentacle thing patrols the halls, icicles jutting out of every side of its body. It's only 1.8x your speed once it spots you, but a CO2 blast barely slows it down instead of freezing it solid the way it would anything else — don't count on buying more than a few seconds with one.

Each of you is carrying a shotgun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can carry up to 3 at a time) and each one loads automatically — let something get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 5 seconds.
