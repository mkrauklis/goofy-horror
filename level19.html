Same room/corridor dimensions and the same clear blue, coral-grown water as the hub room before it — no current this time, just open swimming. Three storm drains sit open somewhere in the halls; stand on one and hold your ground for a full 2.5 seconds to shut it, stepping off part way through and it starts draining back open. Either of you can work any drain, nothing to carry. Shut all three and the exit door unlocks.

What's actually dangerous here isn't one creature, it's ten. A swarm of tiny eels, each barely a third the size of the usual flood eel, every one of them grown over with its own little patch of coral. None of them stalk or hide — there's no detection range to sneak past, no cover to break line of sight. Each one just beelines for whichever player is nearest, all the time, at a flat 1.2x your own speed. One alone is barely a threat. Ten of them funneling you into a corner is a different problem.
