Level 27 — Satellite Array

One big rooftop under a night sky, cluttered with 2x2 crates and rooftop fans. Find the wrench and carry it to the broken satellite dish to fix it — the access hatch unlocks once it's running again. Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

No boardwalks this time — just one large, open rooftop, cluttered with 2x2 maintenance crates and big rooftop exhaust fans, both solid enough to duck behind or block a straight line of sight.

The wrench sits out in the open somewhere on the roof; walk over it to pick it up. Carry it to the satellite dish near the far access hatch and get close enough to fix it — the hatch door unlocks the instant it's running again. Get caught while carrying the wrench and it drops right where you fell, same as any other carried item here.

The thing circling this roof looks like the Reacher from the wing below, but dark grey and winged, actually flying its patrol instead of walking it. It roams at a steady 1x your speed until it spots you, then closes the distance at 1.5x — same sight-based hunting every other monster in this game uses, just faster once it's onto you.
