The Dream

A dark room with no walls, no doors and a checkered floor that doesn't end. Something very large is walking toward you. It is watching.

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

There is nothing to find here. You can walk as far as you like, in any direction, and the floor goes on. It follows, and it gets quicker. You do not escape a dream by outrunning it.
