Level 1 — The Facility

Find all 3 buttons and reach the exit — the blind, wall-crawling creatures only notice you if your flashlight lands on them.

Buttons: 0 / 3 Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

The power's out and it's pitch black beyond your flashlight. Two identical creatures roam the dark independently and can't see you until your own flashlight lands fully on one — once it does, it's 2.5x as fast as you. A network of ventilation ducts threads through the facility as a safe shortcut, and item crates can turn up a radar, a scanner pointing at the nearest unpressed button, a distracting slab of meat, or a CO2 blast that freezes a creature solid.

Each of you is carrying a shotgun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can only hold one at a time) and it loads automatically — let something get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 3 seconds.
