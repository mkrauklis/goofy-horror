Level 9 — The Dig

Search the minecarts scattered along the rail tracks — 3 of the 36 are holding dynamite. Find all 3 and they blow the exit door open. A huge armored caterpillar prowls the halls at 3x your speed.

Dynamite: 0 / 3 Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

Rail tracks run through every hall down here, and 36 stationary minecarts sit on them, abandoned mid-dig. Most are empty. Three are holding dynamite, no way to tell which from the outside — you just have to search them. Find all three and they blow the exit door clean open.

The thing that lives down here is a cousin of the caterpillar from the sinkhole — except this one is twice as thick, five times as long, armor-plated along every segment of its body, pale as bone, and a mouth full of teeth where its cousin just had an eye. It doesn't need to be triggered by a meat vine or anything else: it's simply fast, a flat 3x your own speed, whether it's patrolling or already locked onto you.

A few patches of floor have caved in to bare sand underneath — you can't cross them, but there's always a plank boardwalk laid through somewhere nearby. The caterpillar doesn't need one; it crosses the sand just as easily as solid ground.
