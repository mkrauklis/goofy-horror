Level 18 — The Great Wheel

Clear blue water this time, coral growing everywhere. Find the giant pipe room at the center and have both players hold down the two pressure plates flanking the wheel for 10 seconds straight to turn it and unlock the exit. A giant seaweed-covered eel roams at a full 1.0x your speed with short vision — but spot it and it triples to 3.0x.

A completely different shape from the last two flooded levels — no grid of identical rooms this time. One giant hub room sits at the center with a corridor spoking out to each of four satellite rooms, and the water itself has finally cleared to a real blue, with coral growing wherever the current isn't dragging you anymore — this water's still.

The giant pipe room at the hub is lined wall-to-wall with rusted pipework and holds a single giant wheel at its center, flanked by two pressure plates spaced too far apart for one person to cover both. Both of you have to stand on your own plate and hold it down for a full 10 seconds together — step off even for a moment and the hold starts draining back down. Turning the wheel unlocks the exit door, tucked past the satellite room to the east.

The thing guarding the hub is the biggest of the three eels down here, draped head to tail in trailing seaweed, green from nose to tail. It roams at a full 1.0x your own speed, but its vision is short — it has to be genuinely close to notice you. Once it does, it triples to 3.0x.
