Halloween 3 — The Pumpkin Patch

Find the knife, then carve 3 giant jack-o-lanterns to open the gate — and keep away from the masked thing in the fields.

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

Outside, finally — under a moonless Halloween sky, in a pumpkin patch that runs on for acres. Wooden fences cut it into a patchwork of fields, with gaps between them for you to slip through; lanterns hang from the fence posts, and scarecrows stand watch over the rows. Your old barn is the one place you're safe.

Three giant pumpkins sit in far-off fields, waiting to be carved — but you'll need a knife first, and it's lying somewhere out in the patch (the scanner will point you to it). Stand next to a giant pumpkin and the carving starts on its own: about 4 seconds alone, half that with both of you. Walk away and the cuts slowly heal. Once all three are lit, the gate in the fence swings open.

The Masque patrols the patch: a tentacled mass of flesh in a cracked porcelain mask, both its arms snapped and dangling. It roams at 1x your speed and, once it sees you, speeds up to 1.5x — and carving leaves you standing still in the open. Hay piles are the one place it can't follow you. The screen smears out when it's right on top of you. Item crates are scattered through the fields with the usual radar, scanner, meat, CO2 and the rest.

Each of you is carrying a tranquilizer gun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can carry up to 3 at a time) and each one loads automatically — let something get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 5 seconds.
