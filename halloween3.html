Halloween 3 — The Ductwork

Find the giant vent room and both hold a lever for 5 seconds at the same time to stop the giant fan — then the door opens.

Fan: 0.0 / 5s Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

You're inside the building's ventilation system now: a maze of narrow steel ducts, riveted and grated, lit by the odd caged bulb. Somebody's dropped a piece of Halloween candy here and there on the floor — just now and then. Find your way through the maze to the giant vent room on the far side.

In the middle of that room is an enormous fan, and there's a lever on either side of it. To shut it off, you each stand at a lever and hold it for 5 seconds at the same time — let go of either one and the progress drains away fast. (Playing solo, just the first lever is enough.) You'll see the fan slow as you hold. Once it stops, the door on the far side of the room opens.

You aren't alone in the ducts: two Seepers — slimy purple mutations that drag themselves through the maze at 0.6x your speed, leaving a trail of purple goo behind them. The moment one sees you it lets out a low scream and chases at 1.2x. Holding a lever means standing still in the open. Item crates are scattered through the ducts with the usual radar, scanner, meat, CO2 and the rest.

Each of you is carrying a tranquilizer gun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can carry up to 3 at a time) and each one loads automatically — let something get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 5 seconds.
