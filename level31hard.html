Level 31 — The Way Out

Hard mode. The road is gone — there's no car, no forest, no Multipod. The way out of the facility runs across its roof, and the Being of Pure Chaos is sitting on it: a flying creature covered in hundreds of eyes, its skin and wings stitched together from the textures of every place you've been. It has 1000 HP, and the only thing that hurts it is an antimatter missile: one lands somewhere on the pad every 10 seconds, never more than 3 at a time. Walk into one and it fires itself, homing in for 25 damage.

Boss: 1000/1000 HP Antimatter missiles: 0/3 on the pad Phase: 1 Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

The same rooftop landing pad as Level 30, the whole thing visible on screen at once — but the floor is patched with random textures now (grass, brick, ice, lava, circuit board, carpet, static…), and past the pad's edge there's nothing but open sunrise sky. Nothing on the pad can hurt the boss except the antimatter missiles: the first touches down the moment the fight starts, then one more every 10 seconds, with no more than 3 waiting on the pad at a time. Walk into one and it fires on its own, homing in on the boss at 5x your speed for 25 damage — forty hits in all.

It attacks the way a flying thing would. Charge: wings folded back, it points a random way and barrels off at 2.5x your speed until it hits the pad's edge. Reposition & spin: it glides to a new spot, then spends 5 seconds spinning and caroming around the pad. Shard fling: it tears shards out of its own skin and flings them at whoever's closest at 6x your speed. Dive bomb: it launches off screen, a shadow appears, and it drops onto that spot — the blast sweeps out 8.5 tiles. Every eye on it watches whoever's nearest.

At 500 HP it comes apart. Chaos unbound: random textures start flashing across the sky, its wings swell to 1.5x, every attack gets 1.2x faster, and every second every texture on its skin and wings changes to a new random one. It also gains two attacks: an antimatter barrage (a bolt at whoever's closest once a second for 5 seconds) and a shockwave (it curls into a ball, then snaps open and sends a ring out to 5 tiles). Beat it and the way out opens.
