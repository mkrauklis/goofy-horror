Level 30 — Landing Zone

A rooftop helicopter landing pad, sunrise all around it, the whole pad visible on screen at once. The Garguantuagoyle — a gargoyle 2.5x normal size with one giant eye — has 100 HP. A missile rig touches down somewhere on the pad every 10 seconds; grab it and it locks onto the boss at 5x your speed, 5 damage a hit. At 50 HP its colors invert into its Primal Form, 1.2x faster with two new attacks. If one of you falls, you're down for the rest of the fight — it's only over once everyone has.

A bare rooftop landing pad, painted perimeter stripes and a big white "H" at its center, the whole thing visible on screen at once — nothing to scroll past, every attack readable the moment it starts. There's no wall around it either, just open air and a pink sunrise sky in every direction past the pad's own edge. The only way to hurt the boss is a missile rig that touches down somewhere on the pad every 10 seconds — walk up to it and it fires on its own, homing in on the boss at 5x your own speed until it connects for 5 damage. There's no other way to hurt it.

Four attacks while it's healthy. Charge: it folds its wings back, points in a random direction, and barrels that way at 2.5x your speed until it slams into the pad's own edge. Reposition & spin: it glides to a random spot on the pad over 2 seconds, then spends 5 seconds straight spinning, claws out, caroming around the pad at 1.5x your speed and bouncing off its own edges the whole time. Feather fling: it plants itself and flings a volley of razor feathers at whoever's closest, at 6x your speed. Dive bomb: it launches straight up and off screen, a shadow appears somewhere on the pad, and a moment later it drops out of the sky onto that spot at 3x your speed — the blast sweeps outward to a full 8.5 tiles from the impact, telegraphed by a widening ring around the shadow.

At 50 HP its colors invert, "GARGUANTUAGOYLE" flashes up with "PRIMAL FORM" underneath it, that giant eye starts flashing black and white, its wings swell to 1.5x their size, and every attack above gets 1.2x faster on top of its usual speed. It also gains two new ones. Antimatter barrage: it hurls a bolt of antimatter at whoever's closest once a second for 5 seconds straight, each one flying at 2.5x your speed. Shockwave: it curls into a ball, then snaps back open and sends a shockwave rippling out to 5 tiles in every direction.
