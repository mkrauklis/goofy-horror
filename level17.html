Level 17 — The Current

Flooded again, but the water here is a sickly green and a current drags you left at 0.2x your speed. Find the welding gun and weld all 3 broken pipes (3 seconds each) to open the exit door. A giant, seaweed-draped eel lurks nearly invisible at 1.0x your speed with short vision — spot it and it surges into view, charging at 3.0x.

Flooded like the facility before it, but the water's gone a murky, sickly green, and it isn't still — a steady current drags both of you left the whole time, 0.2x your own speed, whether you're moving or standing still. Swimming against it just costs you speed; swimming with it is free.

Three pipes have ruptured through the rooms. A single welding gun is lying on the floor somewhere — whoever reaches it first carries it for good. Bring it to a pipe and hold your ground for 3 seconds to seal it; wander off partway through and the weld starts draining back down. Seal all three and the exit door unlocks.

The thing living down here this time is the biggest of the three eels in the flood, deep green and draped head to tail in a heavy coat of trailing seaweed. It's 90% transparent at rest -- barely a shadow in the water -- and its vision is short, so it has to be genuinely close before it notices you. Once it does, it surges into view, roaming at a full 1.0x your own speed and charging at 3.0x the instant it spots you.
