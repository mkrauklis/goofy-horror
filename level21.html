Level 21 — The Overgrowth

The biggest facility yet, grass and vines grown up through every floor and wall. Find the shears and cut through the vines choking the one way out — hold still whenever a creature's close, since they hunt by sound alone.

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

The same dark, power-out facility as the very first one, except nothing's been maintained in a long time — grass pushes up through cracked floors in every room, and vines have swallowed whole doorways. It's also the biggest room count on the site by far, a sprawling lattice bigger than even Level 17's own "giant" layout.

A single pair of shears is lying somewhere in the facility. Find them and carry them to the one way out, choked floor to ceiling in vines — get close while still carrying the shears and they cut straight through.

Ten Drifters patrol the dark this time, not two — the same sound-hunting cousin of the Crawler from Level 2, grass sprouting straight out of each one's hide now. Stand still, even right next to one, and it has no idea you're there; move while it's in range and it charges at eight times your speed. Item crates can still turn up a radar, a scanner pointing at the shears (or the vine gate once they're picked up), a distracting slab of meat, or a CO2 blast that freezes a creature solid.

Each of you is carrying a shotgun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can carry up to 3 at a time) and each one loads automatically — let something get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 5 seconds.
