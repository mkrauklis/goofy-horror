Level 20 — The Cistern

A circular arena, flooded teal, grown over with coral and seaweed, fish drifting through it. The Cistern has 50 health — spears spawn every 10 seconds (never more than 3 at once), and walking onto one hits for 2.5 damage. At 25 health it turns DEHYDRATED: every coral patch on its body turns red and doubles in size, and every attack runs 1.1x faster.

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

It looks like the Bog, grown monstrous and gone teal, seaweed and coral growing straight out of its hide -- the coral itself glowing neon blue in phase one. The arena itself is a single round chamber, pitch black but for your own light and a fixed scatter of that same glowing coral, fish drifting through the dark the whole fight.

Four attacks from the start. It can glide to the wall over a 2-second warning, then circle the whole arena's perimeter at 4x your speed for 5 seconds — get caught in its path and that's the end of that attempt. It can fade to almost nothing but a couple of faint ripples, cross the arena unseen, and slam back into view wherever it lands, throwing out a real shockwave a full 6 tiles wide the instant it resurfaces. It can hang back and fire coral balls at 2.2x your speed, two at a time, twice as often as it used to fire. And it can rush to the center at double its usual speed while 25 spots flash a warning around it for 2 seconds, then red coral actually grows in at every one of them, a real hazard rooted there for 3 seconds before it goes again.

Spears are the only way to hurt it — one spawns every 10 seconds, never more than 3 on the floor at once, and walking onto one is the hit. Bring it to 25 health and it goes DEHYDRATED: every coral patch on its body turns red (still glowing) and doubles in size, cracks spread across its whole hide, and every attack above runs 1.1x faster, with a fifth attack joining the rotation — waves splashed out in random directions every 2.5 seconds, for 10 seconds straight, each one as fast as the coral balls. Bring it to 0 and the facility dries up around you.
