Level 5 — The Cinder Pit. One vast, crumbling room, missing floor tiles giving way to bare dirt throughout, and fires burning everywhere you look. Find all 3 buttons together to unlock the exit — and stay well clear of the thing smoldering in the middle: a Crawler burnt black and set alight, eight times the size of a normal one, still very much alive.
