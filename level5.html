Level 5 — The Cinder Pit

Find the bombs scattered around the room and use them to take down the boss, then reach the exit.

Boss: 100% Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

One vast, crumbling room, missing floor tiles giving way to bare dirt throughout, and fires burning everywhere you look. In the middle of it all: a Crawler burnt black and set alight, eight times the size of a normal one, still very much alive. There's no sneaking past it — find the bomb hidden somewhere in the room and get it to go off near the boss to take out 10% of its health, then find the next one. Once it's had ten, the fire finally goes out and the door unlocks. Watch for the ring it telegraphs before it lashes out — get clear before it lands, and it gets faster and more relentless the lower its health gets.
