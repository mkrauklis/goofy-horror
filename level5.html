Level 5 — The Cinder Pit

Survive the boss's attacks, wait for it to stun itself, then land a bomb hit — ten stunned hits and the door unlocks.

Boss: 25/25 HP Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

A much smaller, crumbling room this time, missing floor tiles giving way to bare dirt, fires burning everywhere you look, and a Crawler burnt black and set alight, eight times the size of a normal one, filling most of it. Small vents are punched into the outer wall here and there — the boss can't fit into one and can't reach anything standing inside it, so they're your only real hiding spots. It cycles through six attacks at random for as long as it's alive — a bouncing spin dash, three telegraphed rubble drops, a ring of ground spikes, a triple charge, a spray of quick-fuse bombs, and a blink: a 3-second pulsing warning, then it strobes color for 2 seconds before whipping all its tentacles out twice — they can't punch through a wall, so a vent (or any wall) blocks them outright. Up to three of your own bombs sit on the map at once, always visible on the minimap; the boss has 25 HP, and landing one only really costs it 1 point unless it's mid-stun (every 10th attack, for 10 seconds), when the same hit takes 5. A crate with meat in it stays that way — eat it once and it also refills your sprint bar, then go back and eat it again any time you're running low. Getting caught doesn't respawn you on its own — you're down until your teammate is too, and then it's a full retry.
