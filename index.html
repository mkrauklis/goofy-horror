GOOFY HORROR

Level 1 — The Facility. The power's out and it's pitch black beyond your flashlight. Find all 3 buttons together to unlock the big door out. Something roams the dark slowly and can't see you until your own flashlight lands fully on it — once it does, it's 2.5x as fast as you. Ventilation ducts scattered through the facility are a safe place to hide, and item crates can turn up a radar, a distracting slab of meat, or a CO2 blast that freezes it solid.
