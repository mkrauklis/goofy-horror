GOOFY HORROR

A 2-player local split-screen horror game. Real JavaScript, no build step, no image or sound files — every room, creature, and note of music is generated live in the browser.

Level 1 — The Facility

A huge, pitch-black facility. Two identical wall-crawling creatures patrol independently — find all 3 buttons to unlock the exit.

Level 2 — Blackout

A sprawling lattice of corridors. Two creatures hunt by sound, not sight — find the fuse and the one real fuse box wired to the door.

Level 3 — Sinkhole

The floor is crumbling to dirt. A slow caterpillar creature is harmless on its own — until someone steps on a meat vine.

Level 4 — Overgrown

Same facility, gone to seed, fires flickering in the dark. Power the train with the electrical room's lever — and watch out for the thing that looks like your partner.

Level 5 — The Cinder Pit

One giant, crumbling room on fire from end to end. Find bombs scattered around it to bring down the burnt giant smoldering in the middle, then get out.

Monsterpedia

A field guide to every creature in the game — how it hunts, how fast it is, and what gives it away.
