GOOFY HORROR

A 1 or 2-player local split-screen horror game. Real JavaScript, no build step, no image or sound files — every room, creature, and note of music is generated live in the browser.

Start the Story

Chapter 1

Level 1 — The Facility

A huge, pitch-black facility. Two identical wall-crawling creatures patrol independently — find all 3 buttons to unlock the exit.

Level 2 — Blackout

A sprawling lattice of corridors. Two creatures hunt by sound, not sight — find the fuse and the one real fuse box wired to the door.

Level 3 — Sinkhole

The floor is crumbling to dirt. A slow caterpillar creature is harmless on its own — until someone steps on a meat vine.

Level 4 — Overgrown

Same facility, gone to seed, fires flickering in the dark. Power the train with the electrical room's lever — and watch out for the thing that looks like your partner.

Level 5 — The Cinder Pit

A small room on fire from end to end, dominated by a burnt giant cycling through five attacks. Survive until it stuns itself, then make your bombs count.

Chapter 2

Level 6 — The Undercroft

The floor gave way beneath the boss fight. Cobblestone halls, a statue over every crossing, five armored knights — find and throw all 3 levers to get out for good.

Level 7 — The Mine

Same stone halls, but a fifth of the rooms have lost their floor — stick to the boardwalk. Pull the one lever to send a minecart down its rail; it breaks the door open on its own.

Level 8 — The Deep Vault

Everything built to a cavernous scale, halls and rooms alike. Put out all 5 blue torches to open the vault door — a huge, armored thing watches from twice the normal distance.

Level 9 — The Dig

Rail tracks and 36 stationary minecarts — 3 hold dynamite, and finding them blows the exit open. An armored caterpillar, pale and huge, prowls the halls at 3x your speed.

Level 10 — The Last Knight

A wide arena, fully visible at once, and a knight in dark ruby and iron with a giant sword. Survive its four attacks, stun it, then make your dynamite count.

Chapter 3

Level 11 — The Frost

Icicles hang everywhere, snow drifts past in the dark, and a pale blue tentacle thing patrols the halls. Charge all 3 generators to open the door.

Level 12 — The Mutation

Missing floors force you onto boardwalks. Seat both fuses in the double fuse box to get out -- a purple, three-headed thing hunts by sight, but only once something moves.

Level 13 — The Brood

Big crates and ragged holes punched through the walls break up the usual layout. Five tiny caterpillar things, half the usual size, only notice you if your flashlight lands on one.

Level 14 — The Thaw

Snow and loose wiring cover the floor. Switch on the heater to melt the door open -- two pale green spiders with icicle legs move at twice your speed.

Level 15 — The Thaw's End

A mutation twice the usual size fills a wide snow-covered arena visible all at once. Catch torches to bring down its 25 HP -- hurt it badly enough and it tears open an endless chase down a hallway instead.

Chapter 4

Level 16 — The Flood

Every room down here sits underwater. Turn all 3 red wheels to open the door — an eel-like thing roams at 0.6x your speed, but charges at 1.8x once it spots you.

Level 17 — The Current

Green water, and a current drags you left at 0.2x your speed. Weld all 3 broken pipes to open the door — a giant, seaweed-draped eel lurks nearly invisible at 1.0x with short vision, surging into view and charging at 3.0x once it spots you.

Level 18 — The Great Wheel

Clear blue water and coral, in a hub room layout unlike anything before it. Hold both pressure plates for 10 seconds together to turn the giant wheel — a pale, bump-skinned eel roams at 0.6x, charging at 1.8x once it spots you.

Level 19 — The Drains

Same blue water and coral as the room before it. Hold each of 3 storm drains open for 2.5 seconds to open the door — 10 tiny coral-covered eels, a third the usual size, always swim straight for whoever's nearest at 1.2x your speed.

Level 20 — The Cistern

A circular boss arena, teal water, coral and seaweed, fish drifting through. The Cistern has 50 health — spears spawn every 10 seconds (3 max) for 2.5 damage each. At 25 health it goes DEHYDRATED: red, oversized coral, 1.1x faster attacks, and a new wave-splash barrage.

Chapter 5

Level 21 — The Overgrowth

The biggest facility on the site, grass and vines grown up through every room. Find the shears and cut through the vines choking the one way out — 10 grass-covered Drifters hunt by sound alone, charging at 8x your speed the moment you move too close.
