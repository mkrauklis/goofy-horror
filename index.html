GOOFY HORROR

A 2-player local split-screen horror game. Real JavaScript, no build step, no image or sound files — every room, creature, and note of music is generated live in the browser.

Start the Story

Chapter 1 — The Facility

A huge, pitch-black facility. Two identical wall-crawling creatures patrol independently — find all 3 buttons to unlock the exit.

Chapter 2 — Blackout

A sprawling lattice of corridors. Two creatures hunt by sound, not sight — find the fuse and the one real fuse box wired to the door.

Chapter 3 — Sinkhole

The floor is crumbling to dirt. A slow caterpillar creature is harmless on its own — until someone steps on a meat vine.

Chapter 4 — Overgrown

Same facility, gone to seed, fires flickering in the dark. Power the train with the electrical room's lever — and watch out for the thing that looks like your partner.

Chapter 5 — The Cinder Pit

A small room on fire from end to end, dominated by a burnt giant cycling through five attacks. Survive until it stuns itself, then make your bombs count.

Chapter 6 — The Undercroft

The floor gave way beneath the boss fight. Cobblestone halls, a statue over every crossing, five armored knights — find and throw all 3 levers to get out for good.

Chapter 7 — The Mine

Same stone halls, but a fifth of the rooms have lost their floor — stick to the boardwalk. Pull the one lever to send a minecart down its rail; it breaks the door open on its own.

Chapter 8 — The Deep Vault

Everything twice the usual size, halls and rooms alike. Put out all 5 blue torches to open the vault door — a huge, armored thing watches from twice the normal distance.
