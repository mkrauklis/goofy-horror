DESOLATE APES

A 1 or 2-player local split-screen horror game. Real JavaScript, no build step, no image or sound files — every room, creature, and note of music is generated live in the browser.

Start the Story

Chapter 1

Level 1 — The Facility

A huge, pitch-black facility. Two identical wall-crawling creatures patrol independently — find all 3 buttons to unlock the exit.

Level 2 — Blackout

A sprawling lattice of corridors. Two creatures hunt by sound, not sight — find the fuse and the one real fuse box wired to the door.

Level 3 — Sinkhole

The floor is crumbling to dirt. A slow caterpillar creature is harmless on its own — until someone steps on a meat vine.

Level 4 — Overgrown

Same facility, gone to seed, fires flickering in the dark. Power the train with the electrical room's lever — and watch out for the thing that looks like your partner.

Level 5 — The Cinder Pit

A small room on fire from end to end, dominated by a burnt giant cycling through five attacks. Survive until it stuns itself, then make your bombs count.

Chapter 2

Level 6 — The Undercroft

The floor gave way beneath the boss fight. Cobblestone halls, a statue over every crossing, five armored knights — find and throw all 3 levers to get out for good.

Level 7 — The Mine

Same stone halls, but a fifth of the rooms have lost their floor — stick to the boardwalk. Pull the one lever to send a minecart down its rail; it breaks the door open on its own.

Level 8 — The Deep Vault

Everything built to a cavernous scale, halls and rooms alike. Put out all 5 blue torches to open the vault door — a huge, armored thing watches from twice the normal distance.

Level 9 — The Dig

Rail tracks and 36 stationary minecarts — 3 hold dynamite, and finding them blows the exit open. An armored caterpillar, pale and huge, prowls the halls at 3x your speed.

Level 10 — The Last Knight

A wide arena, fully visible at once, and a knight in dark ruby and iron with a giant sword. Survive its four attacks, stun it, then make your dynamite count.

Chapter 3

Level 11 — The Frost

Icicles hang everywhere, snow drifts past in the dark, and a pale blue tentacle thing patrols the halls. Charge all 3 generators to open the door.

Level 12 — The Mutation

Missing floors force you onto boardwalks. Seat both fuses in the double fuse box to get out -- a purple, three-headed thing hunts by sight, but only once something moves.

Level 13 — The Brood

Big crates and ragged holes punched through the walls break up the usual layout. Five tiny caterpillar things, half the usual size, only notice you if your flashlight lands on one.

Level 14 — The Thaw

Snow and loose wiring cover the floor. Switch on the heater to melt the door open -- two pale green spiders with icicle legs move at twice your speed.

Level 15 — The Thaw's End

A mutation twice the usual size fills a wide snow-covered arena visible all at once. Catch torches to bring down its 25 HP -- hurt it badly enough and it tears open an endless chase down a hallway instead.

Chapter 4

Level 16 — The Flood

Every room down here sits underwater. Turn all 3 red wheels to open the door — an eel-like thing roams at 0.6x your speed, but charges at 1.8x once it spots you.

Level 17 — The Current

Green water, and a current drags you left at 0.2x your speed. Weld all 3 broken pipes to open the door — a giant, seaweed-draped eel lurks nearly invisible at 1.0x with short vision, surging into view and charging at 3.0x once it spots you.

Level 18 — The Great Wheel

Clear blue water and coral, in a hub room layout unlike anything before it. Hold both pressure plates for 10 seconds together to turn the giant wheel — a pale, bump-skinned eel roams at 0.6x, charging at 1.8x once it spots you.

Level 19 — The Drains

Same blue water and coral as the room before it. Hold each of 3 storm drains open for 2.5 seconds to open the door — 10 tiny coral-covered eels, a third the usual size, always swim straight for whoever's nearest at 1.2x your speed.

Level 20 — The Cistern

A circular boss arena, teal water, coral and seaweed, fish drifting through. The Cistern has 50 health — spears spawn every 10 seconds (3 max) for 2.5 damage each. At 25 health it goes DEHYDRATED: red, oversized coral, 1.1x faster attacks, and a new wave-splash barrage.

Chapter 5

Level 21 — The Overgrowth

The biggest facility on the site, grass and vines grown up through every room. Find the shears and cut through the vines choking the one way out — 10 grass-covered Drifters hunt by sound alone, charging at 8x your speed the moment you move too close.

Level 22 — The Greenhouse

A completely different wing of the same overgrown facility — windows along the outer walls finally let real light in. Find the lighter and burn through the vines choking the one way out — 5 brown, stick-covered creatures hunt by sight at a steady 1.1x your speed, each one firing its own shotgun at the closest player every 5 seconds.

Level 23 — The Stalker's Hall

Two big sections joined by one long window-lined hallway full of shelves, same moss and grass as the wing next door. No item to find this time — the exit's already open, just reach it. Three Stalkers roam at 1.8x your speed and give chase at 2.5x your speed the moment one spots you.

Level 24 — The Gearworks

Same two-section layout as the wing next door, now with wildflowers growing through the grass and conveyor belts crossing both rooms. Spin all 3 gears (5 seconds held at each) to open the exit — 2 Sentries hunt by sight just like Level 22's Kindling, 15% slower, each firing its own shotgun at the closest player every 5 seconds.

Level 25 — The Spore Engine

A grassy, window-lined boss arena, the whole thing visible on screen at once. The Cultivator — a round 3x3 robot, real grass on its hull, one giant mushroom on its head — has 25 HP; carry one coal at a time to the furnace on the south wall, 1 damage per coal. At 15 HP it starts rusting and gains two more attacks on top of its first five — and once someone falls, they're down for the rest of the run.

Chapter 6

Level 26 — Rooftops

Twelve rooftops in a 3x4 grid, linked by boardwalks slung over the drops between them, lit only by moonlight and your own flashlight. Get 3 generators running (5 seconds held at each) to open the gate on the exit roof — the Reacher roams the whole rooftop circuit by sight, same as any other hunter here, and launches a missile at whoever it can see every 3 seconds.

Level 27 — Satellite Array

One big open rooftop this time, cluttered with 2x2 crates and rooftop fans. Find the wrench and carry it to the broken satellite dish to fix it — the access hatch unlocks the moment it's running again. A winged, dark grey cousin of the Reacher circles the roof, roaming at a steady 1x your speed until it spots you, then closing at 1.5x.

Level 28 — Power Grid

Three hollow buildings linked by boardwalks over the gaps between them — every one has a real inside and outside, doorways punched straight through the wall. Press all 3 power switches (2 inside, 1 on a ledge) to light every interior and open the exit. Three light grey, four-winged Wardens glide the open sky outside, watching twice the usual range — stand still and they never notice, but move within sight and one locks on for good, diving at 3x your speed until it catches you or you're through a doorway.

Level 29 — Sunrise Escape

Forty small rooftops in an 8x5 grid, same deco as the wing before this one, the sky just starting to go pink instead of pitch dark — and no boardwalks, just a two-way zipline maze (2.2x your speed) with exactly one route to the elevator on the far corner roof. Fifteen tiny, very dark grey Grotesques with oversized wings and one bloodshot red eye apiece patrol a random scatter of the rooftops, individually slow at 0.8x your speed but able to actually fly the gap and come after you once they've spotted you.

Level 30 — Landing Zone

A rooftop helicopter landing pad, sunrise all around it, the whole pad visible on screen at once. The Garguantuagoyle — a gargoyle 2.5x normal size with one giant eye — has 100 HP; a missile rig touches down somewhere on the pad every 10 seconds, 5 damage a hit at 5x your speed once you grab it. At 50 HP its colors invert into Primal Form, 1.2x faster with two new attacks. If one of you falls, you're down for the rest of the fight.

Chapter 7

Level 31 — The Way Out

Broad daylight, out front of the facility, a car in the lot for each of you — and the doors burst open behind you. The Multipod, every monster in the game (bosses included) fused into one swarm with 500 HP, runs you down at 3x walking speed, sprinting to 5x if it falls behind and hurling the game's own projectiles at you every 2.5 seconds — one hit and your car is gone. One straight road through a forest that gets darker the deeper you go, and it doesn't end until the Multipod does. Missiles do 25 damage (12.5 each with two cars).

Holidays

🎃 Halloween 2026

Halloween 1 — The Candy Maze

A Halloween-lit facility turned into a maze: orange floors, jack-o-lanterns, glowing windows. Collect 3 buckets of candy to open the exit — and don't let the pumpkin-headed tentacle thing see you.

Halloween 2 — The Quiet Ward

A hospital with candy strewn across every floor. Crawl the ventilation ducts and switch off all 4 fans to open the exit — while a tentacled ghost that's 95% invisible drifts the wards.

Halloween 3 — The Ductwork

A maze of steel ventilation ducts with a little candy on the floor. Reach the giant vent room and both hold a lever for 5 seconds to stop the giant fan and open the door — past two festering Rotten Ones.

Halloween 4 — The Overgrown Ward

The hospital's overgrown old wing: a sprawl of winding rooms with vines, jack-o-lanterns and big wooden crates. Find the bluelighter and light 3 green pumpkins to open the exit — while a fireball-spitting pumpkin hunts you.

Halloween 5 — The Pumpkin King

A round arena floored with the ward's tiles, vines and glowing jack-o-lanterns. Face a 3x3 pumpkin with a fire in its mouth — stun it, then bite it.
