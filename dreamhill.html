The Hill

A green hill under a very blue sky. The sky is a painted dome and there are little houses dotted about. Every 15 seconds the sky goes out for 10.

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

In the daytime there is nothing to be afraid of. When the sky goes out, eyes open in the dark — huge, wet, realistic ones — and drift about the hill. While you are indoors they only drift. The moment you step outside, every one of them comes for you at six times its drifting speed, and keeps coming until you are back inside a house.

There is a small door in the rim of the sky, to the north. Houses can be entered by their open front doors.
