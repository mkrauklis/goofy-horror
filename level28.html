Level 28 — Power Grid

Three buildings under a night sky, linked by boardwalks slung between their outer ledges. Every building has a real inside and outside — step through a doorway and you're off the ledge and safe. Find and press all 3 power switches (2 inside, 1 out on a ledge) to light up every interior and unlock the exit. Three light grey, four-winged Wardens patrol the open sky outside, watching out to 2x the usual range — stand still outside and they have no idea you're there, but move while one can see you and it locks on for good, diving at 3x your speed until it catches you or you make it through a doorway.

Three separate buildings this time, each one hollow — a solid outer wall wrapped by its own exterior ledge, four doorways punched through that wall. Each doorway opens into its own small, separate room, not one shared hall — there's no way to walk from the room behind the north door to the room behind the east door without stepping back out onto the ledge first. The buildings themselves are linked ledge to ledge by boardwalks slung across the open gaps between them, cluttered with 2x2 maintenance crates and rooftop exhaust fans along the way.

Three power switches keep the whole place running — two tucked inside two of the buildings, one out on a ledge on the far side of the complex. Walk up to one and it presses on contact, no holding required. Press all three and every interior light in every building comes on at once, and the gate on the exit (tucked deep inside the third building) swings open.

The Wardens don't walk a route — they fly one, gliding over the ledges and straight across the open sky between buildings, the same gaps you need a boardwalk to cross. They can only ever see you while you're outside on a ledge, and standing still is enough to go unnoticed even in plain sight — it's moving that gives you away. Let one spot you while you're moving and it locks on for good: no waiting it out, no losing it behind a crate. It'll dive at 3x your speed and keep coming until it catches you or you duck through a doorway, which is the only thing that breaks the lock.
