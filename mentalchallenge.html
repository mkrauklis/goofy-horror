The Mental Challenge

Find all 25 buttons and reach the exit. Every monster in the game is in here with you, and none of them are in colour.

Buttons: 0 / 25 Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

You were not supposed to find this. A level with no colour, nothing to tell friend from foe but the shape of the thing coming at you: all thirty-eight creatures from the Monsterpedia and the grass-covered one from below, each walking a patrol of its own.

There are twenty-five buttons spread through twenty rooms, and the exit door stays shut until every one is pressed. Getting caught only sends you back to the safe room — your buttons stay pressed. Get out, and you earn the title.
