Level 6 — The Undercroft

Find and throw all 3 levers to unlock the exit — five armored knights patrol the stone halls, blind until your flashlight lands on one.

Levers: 0 / 3 Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

The floor gave way under Level 5's boss and dropped you both into whatever this place is — cobblestone halls lit by wall torches, a stone statue standing watch over every crossing. Three of the same blind, wall-crawling creature from Level 1 are down here too, except someone's put them in armor and handed each one a sword: slower than the original (1.2x your speed instead of 2.5x), but there are three of them, and they'll still catch you if your own flashlight lands on one first. Three levers, each trailing a green wire toward the door, need to be thrown before it opens — find all three and get out.
