Level 15 — The Thaw's End

A mutation twice the size of the one before it fills a wide snow-covered arena — the whole thing visible on screen at once. Catch the torches it leaves you no choice but to grab, and watch for the wall that opens once it's hurting badly enough.

Boss: 25/25 HP Torches: 0 live Phase: 1 Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

The mutation from the fuse-box lab out here is bigger, two full body widths across, with patches of ice crusted across its lower half. The arena is wide, open, and buried in snow, with nowhere to hide and nothing in the way — the whole thing fits on screen at once, so every attack is readable the moment it starts. A torch spawns somewhere on the open floor every 10 seconds (up to 5 live at once); walking onto one lands 2 damage against its 25 HP.

Five attacks, cycling forever. Split: a 2-second telegraph as it visibly comes apart, then it tears into two smaller copies, one a lumbering 1.2x your speed, the other a fast 1.6x, both hunting independently until they collapse back into one. Icicle shower: it fires shards at whoever's nearest for 5 seconds straight, each one flying at 2x speed. Wall push: an ice wall rises along one side of the arena, one gap left open, and sweeps across at 1.5x your speed — find the gap before it reaches you. Expand: it plants itself dead center and a hazard ring slowly grows outward — the corners are always far enough away to be safe. Orbit: a ring of icicles spins up around its body and launches outward one at a time as it spins, sweeping shots around the whole arena for 10 seconds straight.

At 15 HP it stops dead and turns bright red, tearing a gate open in the south wall into a holding pen sealed off by a barrier 10 tiles in -- the boss won't appear until someone still standing has walked all the way up to it. The moment one of you does, the barrier drops and it spawns in filling the width of the hallway, standing still for 2.5 seconds (the music changes the moment it actually starts moving) before the real chase begins, down a corridor with big crates to duck behind. It paces itself off you: fall behind and it eases to 0.8x your speed, pull ahead and it surges to 2x, otherwise it holds a steady 1.25x. Icicles keep coming at 2.5x speed the whole way down, and a fresh torch waits every 15 tiles. Land enough of them and a wave of water finally takes it.

Each of you is carrying a shotgun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can carry up to 3 at a time) and each one loads automatically — get close enough to the boss while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, costing it 20% of its speed for 5 seconds.
