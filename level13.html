Level 13 — The Brood

Find all 3 buttons and reach the exit. Big crates and random holes punched clean through the walls break up the usual room layout. Five tiny caterpillar things only notice you if your flashlight lands on one.

Buttons: 0 / 3 Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

Same icicle-and-snow dressing as the frozen halls before it, but solid 2x2 crates are scattered everywhere for cover, and a handful of rooms have a ragged hole punched clean through one wall -- an extra shortcut on top of the normal corridors, not a replacement for them. Find all 3 buttons and reach the exit.

Five tiny caterpillar-like things patrol independently, each about half the size of the usual kind and only 1.1x your speed once they notice you. They're just as blind as their bigger cousins -- nothing happens until your own flashlight lands fully on one.

Each of you is carrying a shotgun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can carry up to 3 at a time) and each one loads automatically — let something get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 5 seconds.
