Halloween 4 — The Graveyard

Turn the great wheel for 10 seconds to open the cemetery gate — with ten of the dead on your heels.

Wheel: 0.0 / 10s Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

A cemetery on Halloween night, wrapped in an iron fence. Rows of gravestones and dead trees fill the plots, four stone mausoleums squat between them, gravel paths cross at lamp posts — and someone has scattered candy over every inch of ground. Your chapel, top-left, is the one place you're safe. Open graves dotted through the plots are good places to hide: nothing can follow you down into one.

In the middle of the cemetery stands an enormous iron wheel. Stand next to it and it turns on its own; keep it turning for 10 seconds in total and the cemetery gate in the east fence swings open. One person at it is enough (a second doesn't make it faster), and if you both let go it slowly winds back down — the scanner points to it. Then it's a run for the gate.

The Risen are what's left of the cemetery's residents: zombies that have grown tentacles, ten of them shuffling the grounds. Every one moves at 1.2x your speed, wandering or hunting, so you can't simply outwalk them — and turning that wheel leaves you standing still. The screen smears out when one is right on top of you. Item crates are scattered through the cemetery with the usual radar, scanner, meat, CO2 and the rest.

Each of you is carrying a tranquilizer gun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can carry up to 3 at a time) and each one loads automatically — let something get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 5 seconds.
