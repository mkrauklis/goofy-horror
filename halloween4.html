Halloween 4 — The Overgrown Ward

Find the bluelighter and light all 3 green pumpkins to open the exit — and watch out for the pumpkin that spits fire.

Bluelighter: find it | Pumpkins: 0 / 3 Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

The hospital's old wing — the same beds, candy-strewn floors and flickering fluorescent lights, but nothing like the ward you remember: a sprawl of rooms of every shape and size joined by winding passages, left to rot and grow. Vines creep across the floor and up the sides of the furniture, jack-o-lanterns sit here and there on the ground, spread far apart, and huge 2x2 wooden crates are stacked all through it — solid, so you'll have to go around.

To get out you need fire. Somewhere in the ward is a bluelighter (it glows blue — the scanner will point to it). Once you have it, find 3 green pumpkins hidden far apart across the building and touch each one to light it. When all three burn blue, the exit door opens.

You aren't alone: the Embergourd, a hulking yellow pumpkin with a smouldering carved face and a nest of writhing tentacles, drags itself through the ward at 0.8x your speed. The moment it sees you it speeds up to 1.3x — and every 5 seconds it spits a fireball at 2.5x your speed. Fireballs burst on walls and crates, so keep cover between you. The safe room is the one place it can't touch you. Item crates are scattered through the ward with the usual radar, scanner, meat, CO2 and the rest.

Each of you is carrying a tranquilizer gun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can carry up to 3 at a time) and each one loads automatically — let something get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 5 seconds.
