Halloween 2 — The Quiet Ward

Turn off all 4 ventilation fans to open the exit — and watch for the ghost you can barely see.

Fans off: 0 / 4 Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

A hospital, after dark, and somebody's emptied a whole Halloween's worth of candy across every floor in the building. Long corridors, a ward of patient rooms off each one, flickering fluorescent lights. The air handling is still running: four loud ventilation fans are wired in behind the walls, and the exit won't unlock until every one of them is switched off.

The fans sit at the heart of the ventilation ducts running around the outside of the building — grated openings at the ends of the corridors, and in the rooms along the edge, lead in. Smaller vent nooks are tucked into the far side of most patient rooms too, all over the ward. Crawl through a duct and the fan inside shuts off the moment you reach it. Vents are the one place the thing in here can't follow you, but it can wait for you at the grate.

That thing is the Hush: a ghost with tentacles and two black eyes, and while it roams the ward at exactly your speed it's 95% transparent — you'll barely see it at all. The instant it spots you it solidifies to almost fully visible and comes at you at 1.9x your speed. A pulsing red edge on the screen means it's close. Item crates are scattered through the ward with the usual radar, scanner, meat, CO2 and the rest.

Each of you is carrying a tranquilizer gun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can carry up to 3 at a time) and each one loads automatically — let something get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 5 seconds.
