Story 2 — Level 2: The Hollow Reach

Find the room with the wheel and crank it — press SPACE at 8 per second for 10 seconds — and the exit door will slowly open. Something pale and thin is roaming. It can't see into the bushes.

The same overgrown facility as before, turned the other way round — dirt where the tiles used to be, roots through the walls, fallen logs, and small animals that bolt when your light finds them. This time there are bushes too, thick and 2×2 tiles wide. Step into one and you can't be seen.

The thing in here is a stick of a creature the colour of old bone, walking on eight long harvestman legs, with a mouth where its eye should be. It wanders at a little faster than your walk. Once it has seen you it runs at more than twice your speed. It cannot see anyone inside a bush, but it will come and look where it saw you last.

Somewhere there is a walled room with a doorway and a big wheel in it. Mash SPACE at 8 presses a second for 10 seconds (slow down and the wheel slips back) and the exit door starts to open. The wheel is loud. Anything roaming nearby will come to see what is making the noise.
