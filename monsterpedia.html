MONSTERPEDIA

Every creature you'll run into, rendered live the same way it is in-game — no screenshots, no sprites. Speeds below are relative to your own walking speed.

The Crawler

Level 1 — The Facility · sight-based

A fleshy, wall-crawling thing with one very realistic eye. Two of them patrol Level 1's corridors independently, each blind to you until your own flashlight lands fully on its eye — the instant it does, it locks on and closes the distance at 2.5x your speed before giving up and going back to patrolling.

The Drifter

Level 2 — Blackout · sound-based

A larger, green cousin of the Crawler that hunts entirely by sound. Stand still, even right beside one, and it has no idea you're there; move while it's in range and it charges at a brutal 8x your speed for a few seconds. Two patrol Level 2 independently, each on its own schedule.

The Caterpillar

Level 3 — Sinkhole · short-range sight

A slow, segmented body dragging itself along at only 0.9x your speed — on its own, harmless. Its eyesight is so short-range it rarely notices you first. The real threat is the meat vines scattered across the ground: trigger one and it rockets to that exact spot at 20x speed.

The Mimic

Level 4 — Overgrown · disguised

Disguises itself as a hazmat-suited person and wanders the halls at close to your own walking pace — genuinely hard to tell apart from your partner at a glance. Get within range and the act drops instantly: it shrinks into a small tentacle creature with a mouth full of teeth instead of an eye, and charges at 2x your speed until it catches someone or loses the trail.
