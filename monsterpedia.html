MONSTERPEDIA

Every creature you'll run into, rendered live the same way it is in-game — no screenshots, no sprites. Speeds below are relative to your own walking speed.

The Crawler

Level 1 — The Facility · sight-based · x2 per level

A fleshy, wall-crawling thing with one very realistic eye. Two of them patrol Level 1's corridors independently, each blind to you until your own flashlight lands fully on its eye — the instant it does, it locks on and closes the distance at 2.5x your speed before giving up and going back to patrolling. Ventilation ducts are the one place it can never follow you.

The Drifter

Level 2 — Blackout · sound-based · x2 per level

A larger, green cousin of the Crawler that hunts entirely by sound. Stand still, even right beside one, and it has no idea you're there; move while it's in range and it charges at a brutal 8x your speed for a few seconds before losing interest. Two patrol Level 2 independently, each on its own schedule, so there's rarely a moment when both are looking the other way.

The Caterpillar

Level 3 — Sinkhole · short-range sight · x1 per level

A slow, segmented body dragging itself along at only 0.9x your speed — on its own, genuinely harmless, and its eyesight is so short-range it rarely notices you first. The real threat is the meat vines scattered across the ground, dense enough that one is almost always somewhere nearby: trigger one and it rockets to that exact spot at 20x speed, arriving in an instant.

The Mimic

Level 4 — Overgrown · disguised · x3 per level

Disguises itself as a hazmat-suited person and wanders the halls at close to your own walking pace — genuinely hard to tell apart from your partner at a glance. Get within range and the act drops instantly: it shrinks into a small tentacle creature with a mouth full of teeth instead of an eye, and charges at 2x your speed until it catches someone or loses the trail. Three of them wander Level 4 independently, each keeping up the act until you're close enough to spook it.

The Crate Lurker

Level 4 — Overgrown · ambush · hides in crates

Not a patrolling creature at all — a trap. A small percentage of Level 4's item crates are secretly occupied instead of holding loot, and the only tell is a jitter in the crate so faint it's easy to miss entirely. Open one and a small purple tentacle thing bursts out and chases whoever's closest at 2.2x your speed until it catches them or gives up after a few seconds, then vanishes for good.

The Ashen One

Level 5 — The Cinder Pit · boss · sight-based

A Crawler like the ones in Level 1 — except this one is burnt black, still on fire, and 8x the size, planted in the middle of a giant burning room. Same rules as its smaller cousin: it can't tell you're there until your flashlight lands fully on it, and even alight and lumbering along at only 2x your speed once it locks on, its sheer reach makes up the difference. It never leaves its arena at the room's center, but it doesn't need to — the whole room is lit by its own fire, and so is everything burning around it.

The Knight

Level 6 — The Undercroft · sight-based · x5 per level

A Crawler fitted out in riveted plate armor and carrying a sword, patrolling the stone halls on foot instead of clinging to a wall. Same tell as its unarmored cousins — it's still blind until your flashlight lands fully on its eye through the gap in its helm — but five of them cover The Undercroft's lattice of rooms independently, each closing in at 1.2x your speed once it spots you.

The Mine Dweller

Level 7 — The Mine · sight-based · x4 per level

Grey all over, with a pair of angular stone wings folded against its back and a mouth full of teeth where its cousins elsewhere have an eye. Its tentacles run 2.5x the normal thickness. Doesn't hunt like anything else in this book: it's tied to the level's own torches, frozen in place the whole 4 seconds they're lit and only moving in the 2 seconds they're dark, at which point it's 10x your speed. It crosses this level's missing-floor rooms as easily as solid ground; you can't follow it there, so the boardwalk across each one is the only way through that doesn't involve it finding you first.

The Vault Guardian

Level 8 — The Deep Vault · sight-based · x1 per level

A pale green cousin of the very first Crawler, bolted into riveted armor plate and blown up to 4x that one's size — same eye, same tell, just much harder to miss and much worse to be seen by. It notices you from 2x the distance a normal creature would, and once it locks on it's 2.5x your speed. Only one patrols the Deep Vault's oversized halls, but at this size and speed, one is plenty.

The Chain Wraith

Level 8 — The Deep Vault · sight-based · x3 per level

Solid black or stark white depending on the one you run into — player-sized, not the Vault Guardian's scale, swinging a chained ball around itself as it moves. Only 1.1x your speed once it locks on, barely faster than walking, but three of them share the Deep Vault's oversized halls with something far bigger, and there's rarely enough room to put much distance between you and either.

The Dig Worm

Level 9 — The Dig · short-range sight · x1 per level

A cousin of Level 3's Caterpillar, dug up from the same stock and grown wrong — 2x as thick, 5x as long, bone-pale, and strapped in riveted armor along every single segment of its body, with a mouth full of teeth where its smaller cousin just has an eye. No slow patrol and sudden charge here, either: it's simply fast all the time, a flat 3x your own speed, whether it's noticed you yet or not.

The Last Knight

Level 10 — The Last Knight · boss · x1 per level

A giant knight in dark ruby and iron, giant sword in hand, filling most of a wide arena you can see all of at once. Four attacks on a loop: a straight charge at 5x your speed, sword pointed forward; its blade turning red hot while it slowly circles, swinging fast; a burst of broken floor shot outward in every direction at 3x; and both tentacles extended all the way out, sweeping around at a lazy 1x. Every 5th attack it's stunned for 10 seconds — the one window a dynamite stick actually costs it real health. Drop it to half and the rubies turn blue, start throwing off fire, and everything above gets 1.5x faster.

The Frost Tentacle

Level 11 — The Frost · sight-based · x1 per level

Pale blue all over, icicles jutting out of every side of its body instead of armor or plates. Only 1.8x your speed once it spots you, but a CO2 blast barely slows it down instead of freezing it solid the way it would anything else in this book — it's used to the cold.

The Mutation

Level 12 — The Mutation · motion-based · x1 per level

Purple all over, three heads grown off one tentacled body — two carry an eye, the third grew in blind. Its sight is entirely motion-based: stand still, even right next to it, and it has no idea you're there. Move while it's in range and it's 2.3x your speed.

The Brood

Level 13 — The Brood · sight-based · x5 per level

Tiny, pale cousins of the very first Crawler — about half that one's size, patrolling independently, five of them at once. Just as blind as the original: nothing happens until your own flashlight lands fully on one, and even then it's only 1.1x your speed.

The Thaw Spider

Level 14 — The Thaw · always hunting · x2 per level

A pale green spider whose legs never softened into joints — each one tapers straight to an icicle point. Two of them patrol the melting halls at 2x your speed, so there's no outrunning one in the open once it's noticed you.

The Frozen Mutation

Level 15 — The Thaw's End · boss · x1 per level

The three-headed mutation from the fuse-box lab, grown to twice its old size with ice crusted across its lower half. Five attacks on a loop: a 2-second telegraph as it visibly comes apart, then tearing into two identical hunting copies, both at 1.2x your speed; a 5-second shower of icicles at 1.5x; an ice wall rising from one random arena edge (never the edge whoever's closest is already backed up against), the room lighting up with blue torches, and sweeping straight across at 1.5x toward its one gap; planting itself center-stage while a hazard ring slowly grows outward, corners always safe; and spinning up a ring of icicles around its own body that launch outward one at a time, sweeping the whole arena for 10 seconds. 25 HP, and the only way to land a hit is a torch -- one spawns on the floor every 10 seconds, up to 5 at once, each worth 2 damage.

Click its picture for phase 2: at 15 HP it stops dead and turns bright red, tearing a gate open into a hallway pen sealed by a faint, almost invisible blue barrier 10 tiles in -- the moment everyone still standing has walked all the way up to it, the barrier drops and the boss spawns in, filling the whole corridor, standing still for 2.5 seconds before a faster chase theme kicks in and the real chase actually begins, pinned to the center of the corridor the entire way down. It paces off its own distance to you -- 0.8x your speed within 6 tiles, 2.1x past 12 tiles behind, a steady 1.25x otherwise -- but a hard 2.5x the moment it's fully off either player's screen, until it's back in view. A lone icicle fires every 3 seconds at 2.625x speed and shatters against the hallway's big crates -- crates the boss itself just smashes straight through -- and a fresh torch waits every 15 tiles.

The Flood Eel

Level 16 — The Flood · sight-based · x1 per level

Long, dark teal, finned along its whole spine and both sides of its head, a mouth instead of an eye where it tracks you. It roams the flooded halls at a lazy 0.6x your own speed, but the instant it spots you across the open water it's 1.8x your speed, and there's nowhere dry to run.

The Bog

Level 17 — The Current · sight-based · x3 per level

Three of them patrol the flood, the biggest of the eels down here, deep green and draped head to tail in a heavy coat of trailing seaweed. Its body is completely invisible at rest -- the seaweed drifting through the water is the only thing there is to see -- and it roams at a full 1.0x your own speed, faster than its cousins elsewhere in the flood. Its vision is short, so it has to be genuinely close before it notices you. Once it does, its whole body surges into view and triples to 3.0x.

The Current Eel

Level 18 — The Great Wheel · sight-based · x1 per level

A cousin of the Flood Eel, pale sickly blue instead of dark teal, with strange raised bumps scattered across its whole body. Same build otherwise -- finned, a mouth instead of an eye -- and the same pace: a lazy 0.6x your own speed roaming, 1.8x the instant it spots you.

The Luminescent

Level 18 — The Great Wheel · sight-based · x1 per level

A long, dark purple eel that lives in the black water at the far end of the channel, a string of glowing lights down both sides of its body marking where it is. It cruises the dark water at 1x your speed, but the moment it spots your boat it sprints at 3x — as fast as the boat itself — and bites it out from under you.

The Drain Swarm

Level 19 — The Drains · always-hunting · x10 per level

Ten of them, not one -- each barely a third the size of the Flood Eel, every body grown over with its own little patch of coral. None of them stalk or hide: there's no detection range, no line of sight to break. Every single one just swims straight for whoever's nearest, all the time, at a flat 1.2x your own speed. It's not any one of them that gets you.

The Cistern

Level 20 — The Cistern · boss · x1 per level

Looks like the Bog, grown monstrous and gone teal, both seaweed and coral growing straight out of its hide this time instead of just one or the other. Fifty health, three attacks -- a 4x-speed lap of the whole arena wall, a near-invisible vanish-and-slam, and a coral-ball barrage -- and spears are the only thing that hurts it. Click for phase 2.

The Grass Drifter

Level 21 — The Overgrowth · sound-based · x10 per level

The same cousin of the Crawler as Level 2's own Drifter, grass sprouting straight out of its hide now instead of bare skin. Ten of them patrol this one, not two, each hunting entirely by sound — stand still, even right beside one, and it has no idea you're there. Move while it's in range and it charges at a brutal 8x your own speed before losing interest.

The Kindling

Level 22 — The Greenhouse · sight-based · x5 per level

Another cousin of Level 1's own Crawler, sticks jutting out of its hide and its whole body gone brown. No slow patrol and fast charge here -- it's always closing at a steady 1.1x your speed the instant it can see you. Worse, it's carrying a shotgun of its own: every 5 seconds it fires at whichever of you it can currently see that's closest, the bullet itself covering ground at 3x your speed.

The Stalker

Level 23 — The Stalker's Hall · sight-based · x3 per level

A gaunt, shadow-dark thing with one watching eye, patrolling at a slow 1.8x your speed. The moment its eye catches you in its sightline, that eye flares hot red and it gives chase at 2.5x your speed until it loses you again.

The Sentry

Level 24 — The Gearworks · sight-based · x2 per level

A tentacled creature grown in around salvaged machinery, gears studding its hide and one red lens for an eye. Same traits as Level 22's Kindling, just 15% slower: it closes in at a steady pace the instant it can see you, and every 5 seconds it fires its own shotgun at whichever of you it can currently see that's closest, the blast itself covering ground at 3x your speed.

The Cultivator

Level 25 — The Spore Engine · boss · x1 per level

A round 3x3 robot, real grass growing out of its hull, one giant mushroom sprouting from its head. 25 HP, and the only way to hurt it is coal fed into the furnace on the arena's south wall — the freeze item does nothing against it, and you can only ever carry one coal at a time. Five attacks: a gear launched at whoever's closest every 2 seconds for 10 seconds straight, ricocheting off the walls at 2x your speed; a head-shake that scatters ~20 spores out across the whole arena, each landing and swelling into a mushroom before bursting in a blast about 2 tiles across; both extendo-arms planting into the ground and tunneling after whoever's nearest at 1.3x your speed, leaving harmless mounds behind the hand itself; a cannon from its back firing 8 rockets that explode on the first thing they touch; and its right arm, a magnet instead of a claw, locking onto whoever's nearest and hauling them in at 0.8x your speed for 7 seconds — getting dragged into contact with it is just as lethal as anything else here.

At 15 HP its grass turns yellow and its plating starts rusting, the spore bloom now throws 10 extra spores on top of the usual 20, and it gains two more attacks: a 5-tile jet of fire breathed in whatever direction it's facing, and a stalk where every window seals shut over 1 second, the mushroom flashes pitch black to neon green every 2 seconds, and it bears down on whoever's nearest at a steady 1x your speed while your own flashlight shrinks to barely a tile around you.

Fall and you're down for the rest of the run — the fight only ends once every one of you has.

The Reacher

Level 26 — Rooftops · x1 per level

A mottled violet blob with a single slit red eye, ringed with short tentacles that never stop waving as it moves. It patrols the whole rooftop circuit the same sight-based way everything else in this game hunts — roaming until it spots you, then closing in, and catching you if you keep moving within reach of it. It also carries a missile launcher, firing a real, dodgeable projectile at whoever it can currently see every 3 seconds, not an instant hit.

The Gargoyle

Level 27 — Satellite Array · x1 per level

The same mottled-blob family as the Reacher one wing over, but dark grey and winged, actually airborne instead of rooted or walking — its own shadow drifts across the roof a beat behind it. It hunts by sight like everything else here, but at a dual speed: a steady 1x your speed while it's just patrolling, jumping to 1.5x the instant it spots you.

The Warden

Level 28 — Power Grid · x3 per level

The same stubby-tentacled family as the Reacher and the Gargoyle, but light stone grey and four-winged, true fliers gliding the open sky instead of rooted or walking a route. They only ever hunt while you're caught out on a ledge, watching twice as far as most of this book's sight-based hunters do — but standing still is enough to go unnoticed even in plain view, since it's movement that actually catches their eye. Get spotted while moving and that's it: no grace period, no losing it behind cover. It folds in and dives at a brutal 3x your speed, and keeps coming until it catches you or you make it through a doorway — the only thing that breaks the lock.

The Grotesque

Level 29 — Sunrise Escape · x15 per level

The runt of the stubby-tentacled family that gave us the Reacher, the Gargoyle, and the Warden — barely half your size, hunched and very dark grey, with a pair of wings comically oversized for a body this small, and a single bloodshot red eye. It patrols on foot, slow at only 0.8x your own speed and trivial to outrun one at a time — but unlike everything else in this family, the wings genuinely work: spot it from across a gap and it flies straight at you, no roof in between required. There are fifteen of them scattered thin across the rooftop maze, hunting independently, and getting boxed in by three or four at once, from the air as easily as the ground, is a real way to run out of directions to go.

Garguantuagoyle

Level 30 — Landing Zone · boss · x1 per level

The same stubby-tentacled, winged family as the Reacher, the Gargoyle, the Warden, and the Grotesque, grown to 2.5x their usual size and glaring out of one giant eye instead of the family's small one. 100 HP, and the only way to hurt it is a missile rig that touches down somewhere on the landing pad every 10 seconds — grab it and it homes in on the boss at 5x your own speed for 5 damage a hit. It charges, repositions and spins with its claws out (caroming around the pad at 1.5x your speed the whole time it's spinning), flings razor feathers at 6x your speed, and dive bombs a shadow it drops onto from off screen for a blast that sweeps out a full 8.5 tiles. At 50 HP its colors genuinely invert, flashing up as its Primal Form — its giant eye starts flashing black and white, its wings swell to 1.5x, everything about it runs 1.2x faster, and it adds an antimatter barrage and a curl-and-release shockwave on top of its first four attacks.

The Multipod

Level 31 — The Way Out · boss · x1 per level

Every creature you've met, bosses included, fused into one screaming swarm — and every member of it is that creature's own model: the Crawler's one eye, the Mimic's hazmat disguise, the Cistern's coral, the Last Knight's armor, the Garguantuagoyle's giant eye. 500 HP, and it doesn't hunt by sight or sound: it just runs down the rearmost car on one straight road at 3x your walking speed, sprinting up to 5x the moment it falls behind. Every 2.5 seconds it throws one of the game's own projectiles (a gear, a rocket, an icicle, a feather, antimatter, a coral ball, a spore, a chain ball, a slug, a chunk of floor) at 6x your speed, aimed where you're about to be — one hit destroys a car. Missiles home in on it at 5x for 25 damage apiece (12.5 each with two cars) and every hit knocks it back. Let it catch the last car and that's the end.

The Hollow Jack

Halloween 1 — The Candy Maze · sight-based · x1 per level

A mass of tentacles wearing a hollowed-out pumpkin, eyeholes carved straight through it. It wanders the maze at only 0.8x your speed, but it has the best eyes of anything in the facility: anywhere in a straight, unobstructed line from it — no matter how far down the corridor, and no matter where your flashlight is pointing — it spots you and hunts you down at 1.6x your speed. Break line of sight, or duck into a ventilation duct, which it can never follow you into.

The Hush

Halloween 2 — The Quiet Ward · sight-based · x1 per level

A ghost with a tangle of pale tentacles and two black eyes, drifting the hospital wards. While it roams — at exactly 1x your speed — it is 95% transparent, nearly impossible to pick out of the dark. The moment it spots you it solidifies to just 10% see-through and runs you down at 1.9x, then fades back out once it loses you. (This portrait shows it the way it looks while hunting.) Ventilation ducts are the one place it can't follow.

The Seeper

Halloween 3 — The Ductwork · sight-based · x2 per level

A slimy purple mutation: a lumpy, glistening mass with too many mismatched eyes and a wide wet mouth, dragging itself through the ventilation ducts on blobby pseudopods and leaving a trail of purple goo that slowly fades. Two of them ooze through the maze at 0.6x your speed — but the moment one sees you it lets out a low scream and comes after you at 1.2x.

The Embergourd

Halloween 4 — The Overgrown Ward · sight-based · x1 per level

A hulking pumpkin with a smouldering carved face, dragging itself through the overgrown ward on a writhing nest of green vine-tentacles that grip the walls as it goes. It trundles along at 0.8x your speed, but the moment it sees you it speeds up to 1.3x — and every 5 seconds its mouth blazes open and it spits a fireball at 2.5x your speed. Fireballs burst against walls and crates, so keep cover between you.

The Pumpkin King

Halloween 5 — The Pumpkin King · boss · x1 per level

A huge 4.5x4.5 orange pumpkin with a carved face and a fire burning in its mouth, held up on a nest of green vine-tentacles, leaving a trail of embers that slowly fades. 50 HP, and you can only hurt it by walking up and hitting it (5 damage) while it's stunned — after every 5th attack, for 7.5 seconds, its mouth-fire guttering down to embers. Its attacks: a spiral that spits double-size fireballs at 5x speed for 10 seconds; a roar followed by a 1.2x chase for 10 seconds; ten eruption patches that flash red for a second before fire shoots up; and the vine whirl, where it ricochets off the walls at 2x speed for 10 seconds swinging 5-tile vines round itself. At 30 HP it flourishes — it turns green, grows a bigger nest of leafy vines, its fire burns blue — and adds the thorn burst (it curls up all its vines, then a shockwave of thorn vines bursts out 6 tiles in every direction) and clouds of poisonous spores that kill you if you stay in one for 2.5 seconds, or just slow you by 30% for 5 seconds if you get out in time. (Click the portrait to see it flourished.)
