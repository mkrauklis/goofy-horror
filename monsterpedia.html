MONSTERPEDIA

Every creature you'll run into, rendered live the same way it is in-game — no screenshots, no sprites. Speeds below are relative to your own walking speed.

The Crawler

Level 1 — The Facility · sight-based · x2 per level

A fleshy, wall-crawling thing with one very realistic eye. Two of them patrol Level 1's corridors independently, each blind to you until your own flashlight lands fully on its eye — the instant it does, it locks on and closes the distance at 2.5x your speed before giving up and going back to patrolling. Ventilation ducts are the one place it can never follow you.

The Drifter

Level 2 — Blackout · sound-based · x2 per level

A larger, green cousin of the Crawler that hunts entirely by sound. Stand still, even right beside one, and it has no idea you're there; move while it's in range and it charges at a brutal 8x your speed for a few seconds before losing interest. Two patrol Level 2 independently, each on its own schedule, so there's rarely a moment when both are looking the other way.

The Caterpillar

Level 3 — Sinkhole · short-range sight · x1 per level

A slow, segmented body dragging itself along at only 0.9x your speed — on its own, genuinely harmless, and its eyesight is so short-range it rarely notices you first. The real threat is the meat vines scattered across the ground, dense enough that one is almost always somewhere nearby: trigger one and it rockets to that exact spot at 20x speed, arriving in an instant.

The Mimic

Level 4 — Overgrown · disguised · x3 per level

Disguises itself as a hazmat-suited person and wanders the halls at close to your own walking pace — genuinely hard to tell apart from your partner at a glance. Get within range and the act drops instantly: it shrinks into a small tentacle creature with a mouth full of teeth instead of an eye, and charges at 2x your speed until it catches someone or loses the trail. Three of them wander Level 4 independently, each keeping up the act until you're close enough to spook it.

The Crate Lurker

Level 4 — Overgrown · ambush · hides in crates

Not a patrolling creature at all — a trap. A small percentage of Level 4's item crates are secretly occupied instead of holding loot, and the only tell is a jitter in the crate so faint it's easy to miss entirely. Open one and a small purple tentacle thing bursts out and chases whoever's closest at 2.2x your speed until it catches them or gives up after a few seconds, then vanishes for good.

The Ashen One

Level 5 — The Cinder Pit · boss · sight-based

A Crawler like the ones in Level 1 — except this one is burnt black, still on fire, and 8x the size, planted in the middle of a giant burning room. Same rules as its smaller cousin: it can't tell you're there until your flashlight lands fully on it, and even alight and lumbering along at only 2x your speed once it locks on, its sheer reach makes up the difference. It never leaves its arena at the room's center, but it doesn't need to — the whole room is lit by its own fire, and so is everything burning around it.
