The Clouds

You are somewhere very high up, and very bright. The maze has no roof. Something with a great many eyes is patrolling it. Find five pieces of the book and the gate will open.

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

The pieces lie in the dead ends. It follows the corridors the way you do, and it quickens with every piece you find. If it reaches you, you start again from the beginning — but the pieces you have already found stay found. When the book is whole, the gate at the far end opens.
