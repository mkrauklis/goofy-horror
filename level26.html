Level 26 — Rooftops

A whole grid of rooftops under a night sky, boardwalks slung across the gaps between them. Get 3 generators running (hold each for 5 seconds) to open the gate on the far roof. The Reacher prowls the whole circuit by sight like anything else here, and fires a missile at whoever it can see every 3 seconds.

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

No hallways this time — twelve separate rooftops laid out in a 3x4 grid, each one only reachable by crossing a narrow wooden boardwalk slung over the drop to the next building. Every roof connects to at least two others, so there's always more than one way across.

Three generators are scattered across three of the far rooftops. Stand at one for a full 5 seconds to get it running; step away and it winds back down. Get all three going and the gate on the exit roof swings open.

The Reacher roams the whole rooftop circuit, same sight-based hunting model as everything else here — patrolling until it spots you, then closing in. It also carries a missile launcher of its own, firing at whoever it can currently see every 3 seconds: a real, dodgeable projectile, not an instant hit, so watch for the flare and get out of its path.
