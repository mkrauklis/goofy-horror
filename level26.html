Level 26 — Rooftops

Six rooftops, boardwalks slung across the gaps between them. Get 3 generators running (hold each for 5 seconds) to open the gate on the far roof. Something lives at the center of it all — a single tentacled thing, rooted in place, its reach stretching clean across the gaps to whichever roof you're standing on.

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

No hallways this time — six separate rooftops laid out in a grid, each one only reachable by crossing a narrow wooden boardwalk slung over the drop to the next building. Every roof connects to at least two others, so there's always more than one way across.

Three generators are scattered across three of the far rooftops. Stand at one for a full 5 seconds to get it running; step away and it winds back down. Get all three going and the gate on the exit roof swings open.

The thing living here doesn't chase — it doesn't have to. It sits rooted at the center of the rooftops, and every few seconds one of its tentacles rears up, winds back, and snaps clean across the gap toward whichever roof you're standing on. Watch for the ring it telegraphs before it lands and you have time to move; stand still in it and that's the end of you. Wander too close to the thing itself and its shorter, idle tentacles will do the job instead.
