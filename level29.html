Level 29 — Sunrise Escape

Four bare rooftops in a row, the sky just starting to go pink at the edges instead of pitch dark. No boardwalks this time — find all 3 ziplines and ride them (at 2.2x your speed) across the open gaps between roofs, then get everyone into the elevator on the far roof to escape. Fifteen tiny, very dark grey Grotesques with bloodshot red eyes swarm the rooftops — slow on their own at 0.8x your speed, but there's a lot of them.

Same bare rooftop deco as the wing before this one — 2x2 maintenance crates and rooftop fans, no walls, no way inside — but four separate roofs this time, each one a real gap of open air from the next. Nothing bridges them. The sky's gone from black to a deep, warm pink along the horizon; it's dim, not dark, your own flashlight filling in the difference.

Each gap has exactly one zipline strung across it, post on this roof, landing spot on the next. Walk up to a post and it fires automatically, no button to press — you're carried straight across at 2.2x your own speed and can't be caught mid-ride. They only run one way; there's no walking back across a gap once you've crossed it.

The Grotesques are the smallest things in this book — barely half your size, dark grey all over with one bloodshot red eye apiece — and individually slower than you at 0.8x your speed, easy to outrun one at a time. There are 15 of them spread across the four roofs, though, patrolling independently, and getting boxed in by several at once is a real problem a single slow chaser never would be.
