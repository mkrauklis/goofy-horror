Level 16 — The Flood

Every room down here sits underwater. Turn all 3 red wheels scattered through the halls to open the exit door. An eel-like thing roams the flooded rooms at 0.6x your speed — spot it and it charges at 1.8x.

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

The whole facility down here has flooded, every room and hall under a few feet of dark water. Three red wheel valves are scattered through the rooms — walk up to each one to turn it. Turn all three and the exit door unlocks. Strands of seaweed sway along the floor, and the water itself ripples out behind anything that moves through it, you and the thing living down here both.

You're each fitted with a pair of flippers for the swim. The thing that lives in these flooded halls is eel-like — long, finned, a mouth instead of an eye where it tracks you. It roams at a lazy 0.6x your own speed until it spots you, then charges at 1.8x.
