Level 12 — The Mutation

Find both fuses and seat them in the double fuse box to open the door. Missing floor tiles force you onto boardwalks in a few rooms. A purple, three-headed thing hunts by sight — but only once something moves.

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

Same icicle-and-snow dressing as the frozen halls before it, but a few rooms here have lost their floor entirely — the only way across is the boardwalk laid through each one. Two fuses are scattered through the level; carry both to the double fuse box and get them seated to unlock the door. Item crates can turn up a radar, a scanner pointing at whichever fuse still needs attention, a distracting slab of meat, or a CO2 blast.

A purple, three-headed mutant tentacle thing patrols the halls -- two heads carry an eye, the third grew in blind. It's 2.3x your speed once it locks on, but its sight is entirely motion-based: stand still, even right next to it, and it has no idea you're there.

Each of you is carrying a shotgun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can carry up to 3 at a time) and each one loads automatically — let something get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 5 seconds.
