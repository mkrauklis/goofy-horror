Level 14 — The Thaw

Find the heater and switch it on to melt back the snow and unlock the exit. Two icicle-legged spiders are twice as fast as you — stay out of their reach.

Heater: off Door: locked Time: 0:00.0 Best: --:--

Player 1 (orange, left): W A S D  ·  Player 2 (green, right): Arrow keys · sound plays after your first key press

Most of the floor out here is buried under snow, with loose wiring strung across a handful of rooms and solid 2x2 crates stacked up for cover. Find the heater switch and turn it on — it melts back about a tenth of the snow on the ground and unlocks the door to the exit. There's no second switch and no puzzle beyond finding it.

Two spider-like things stalk the halls, pale green with long legs that taper to sharp icicle points instead of the usual joints. They move at 2x your speed, so there's no outrunning one in the open — break line of sight or use what cover the crates give you.

Each of you is carrying a shotgun, empty. Find a shell in a crate (you can carry up to 3 at a time) and each one loads automatically — let something get close while it's hunting you and it goes off on its own, stunning it for 5 seconds.
